## Line and rendering direction

- Clean Japanese anime model-sheet readability, with clear front, three-quarter,
  side, and back views.
- **Linework - APPROVED / PASS:** clean, delicate, fine ink contours with thin,
  crisp edges and controlled line weight; keep interior details and individual
  hair strands precise and readable at normal viewing size.
- **Rendering:** Japanese horror ink / manga wash with selective dry-brush
  texture, sparse cool-gray wash, restrained screentone, and small pooled black
  shadows.
- Off-white paper and deliberate negative space; unease should come from
  stillness, concealment, and shadow rather than photorealistic detail.
- Keep the character readable as an anime design first and horror illustration
  second.
- **Ayame Hirasaka version 2 exception:** use clean shoujo / light-novel 2D
  anime rendering in neutral grayscale, with soft cel shading and bright, gentle
  tonal values. Do not apply the horror wash, dry-brush texture, or heavy pooled
  shadows to her approved pre-tragedy design; keep her human, warm, and quietly
  compassionate.

## Shion Madoka — murderer guise visual invariants

- Very slim, tall adult male silhouette; preserve the identity established in
  the approved Shion references.
- Faded off-white bridal kimono or old ceremonial outer layer, with broad
  sleeves, layered collar, weathered fabric, and a high neck wrap.
- Pale aged wooden bridal mask carved with a calm young woman's face.
- Thin gloves and small bells at the ankle or kimono hem.
- Black wig gathered into a low ponytail at the nape, with a few loose damp
  strands. This is the approved visual interpretation of the canon's long black
  wig.
- Black ink, cool gray, aged ivory, and dirty white as the dominant palette;
  dark crimson is a minor cord or ribbon accent only.
- The masked form is the primary horror silhouette. An unmasked open-eyed
  study is a rare human reveal, not the default expression; eyes must remain
  calm and human, never glowing or demonic.

The approved summer outfits reflect the university-break trip. Avoid styling the
protagonists as high-school students. Risa's asymmetric earrings and Nao's
single small hoop are distinct approved design details.

## Avoid

Photorealism, cinematic realism, painterly watercolor, 3D rendering, high-school
uniform styling for the university-age protagonists, modern
tactical or leather-assassin clothing, fantasy armor, pristine bridal white,
muscular proportions, cute/chibi proportions, high ponytails, fully loose
long hair, glowing eyes, demonic anatomy, gore, weapons, readable labels,
logos, and watermarks.

## Canon boundary

The story files establish the old bridal mask, faded white kimono or covering,
gloves, bells, long black wig, and neck covering as Shion's murderer costume.
They also establish that the costume began as a folklore-ceremony prop and was
later reused to conceal his identity. The low ponytail and the rare open-eyed
reveal are approved visual-direction decisions recorded here; they are not
claimed as pre-existing prose facts.
