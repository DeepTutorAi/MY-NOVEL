# Machine & Vessel Mechanics: Project "AETHEL Core"

## 1. The Technology: "AETHEL Core" (Neural-Environmental Harmonizer)
- **Public Corporate Claim**: Presented at the Day 1 Gala as a revolutionary bio-electromagnetic stabilizer designed to eliminate seasickness, reduce passenger stress, and induce deep restorative sleep using Extremely Low Frequency (ELF) fields paired with ionized atmospheric modulation.
- **Hidden Reality**: Built using experimental bio-semiconductors and resonance crystals derived from suppressed neurological research. When operated inside a massive closed steel cruise hull on open water, the vessel acts as a **giant cavity resonator**, amplifying standing waves that strip away human cognitive filters and project subconscious fears into shared reality.

## 2. Engineering Cascade & Signal Blackout (Days 2–3)

### 2.1 Day 2: Early Instability & First Exposure
- **Noon–Evening (Day 2)**: Initial minor signal instability begins (choppy satellite and radio connections observed from the bridge, rationalized as weather).
- **Early Exposure (Day 2)**: Ota Yuichi, spending the longest shifts near the core, suffers acute neurological symptoms first—migraines, delirium, disorientation. Returning coworkers mistake this for severe illness and take him to rest/infirmary, saving him from the Day 3 machine bay collapse.

### 2.2 Day 3: Midday Blackout & Engineering Collapse
- **Midday/Noon (Day 3)**: External communications degrade rapidly and drop to 0% mid-call (severing Nao's check-in with Ryoya).
- **Night 3 Runaway**: Under sustained operation and overheating, the remaining engineering team suffers acute psychosis inside the bay, smashing controls and perishing while arc flashes weld relays shut and blast doors seal the core pit. With no technicians left to throttle the system, the steel hull becomes a continuous broadband harmonic jammer.
- **Unseen Casualty**: Shiraishi Shinji dies alone in a secluded Deck 2 service corridor breaker alcove during Day 3.

## 3. Differential Symptom Spectrum & Mass Panic

### 3.1 Four Tiers of Psychological Manifestation
1. **Cognitive Resistance**: Individuals with prior psychological conditioning or trauma immunity (e.g., Nao) or natural sensitivity (e.g., Hina) maintain partial lucidity through Day 3–4.
2. **Stupor & Catatonia**: Victims become unresponsive, withdrawing into corners or locking themselves away.
3. **Acoustic Agony & Fatal Despair**: Victims suffering unbearable high-frequency skull ringing and auditory hallucinations jump overboard into the dark ocean to make the noise stop.
4. **Violent / Calculating Psychosis**:
   - *Panic-Induced Self-Defense (Day 3)*: Ordinary passengers trapped in high-resonance zones hallucinate loved ones or neighbors as lethal threats and kill out of sheer terror.
   - *Mob Violence (Day 4)*: Passengers and crew hallucinate monsters or threats among one another.
   - *Cold Psychosis (Ota Yuichi)*: Warped into a quiet, intelligent predator utilizing ship maintenance blind spots.

### 3.2 The "First Spark" Mob Catalyst (Day 4 Evening)
- With no firearms aboard, mass panic requires an undeniable public shock to shatter social order.
- During a tense confrontation in the Grand Atrium at dusk on Day 4, an acutely psychotic VIP passenger violently stabs a crew member and leaps from the 4th-tier balcony onto the marble floor in full view of the crowd.
- The resulting stampede and bloodlust ignite an overnight hell across the vessel until dawn on Day 5.

## 4. Failed Shutdown, Sinking & Portable EPIRB Escape (Night 4 → Day 5)
- **Location**: Installed in the **Aft Marine Research Bay (Deck 2)**, adjacent to the **Deck 2 Aft Service Corridor**.
- **Climax Obstacles (Night 4)**:
  - Takai provides industrial ear protection to Nao's group, offering partial acoustic mitigation without blocking the electromagnetic field entirely.
  - Fires, explosions, and structural damage cut off routes and make shutting down or jettisoning the core physically impossible for ordinary survivors.
  - In the **Deck 2 Aft Service Corridor**, Nao scouts ahead while Hina watches him; Reika discovers Shinji's Day 3 corpse, attacks Hina with an emergency industrial flashlight, is restrained by Nao, and all three survive Ota Yuichi's ambush when injured Hina incapacitates him.
- **Flooding, Sinking & Signal Escape**:
  - Seawater floods Deck 2, partially disrupting the steel hull's cavity resonance (easing symptoms slightly for survivors, though the core itself is not 100% destroyed and severely affected individuals remain deranged).
  - Survivors evacuate via lifeboats before the vessel lists and sinks permanently into the ocean abyss on Day 5 morning, taking the AETHEL Core down with it.
  - First Officer Nakamura carries the bridge's detachable **Portable EPIRB** into the lifeboats; once the boats drift clear of the sinking hull's jamming radius, the beacon transmits cleanly to satellites, allowing the JCG to reach survivors 3–4 hours after dawn.
