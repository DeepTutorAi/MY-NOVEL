# ⏳ Project Timeline: Backstory & Narrative Chronology

This timeline tracks historical milestones, the active progression of the novel, and the far-future epilogue.

---

## 🏛️ Backstory / Historical Timeline

| Relative Time | Event Name | Key Entities | Details & Narrative Relevance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Year -1000** | The Ocean Lift | The Ascendant Engineers | The Sky-Dam Engine is built to lift the oceans to harness energy and prevent human extinction. The Dead Parliament consciousness is archived. |
| **Year -19** | Cael's Birth | Cael | Cael Arvane is born in the mountain city of Elaris. |
| **Year -14** | Nio's Birth | Nio, Cael | Nio Arvane is born five years after Cael. The brothers grow up together in Elaris. |
| **Year -10** | The Forbidden Touch | Cael, Nio, Isolde, Rovan, Father Odran | Nio touches the forbidden rain. Mutation begins. Isolde is exposed to brine-spore-laden fluid while treating him. Father Odran (then Inquisitor) signs Nio's transfer order to the White-Silt Bastion. |
| **Year -10, later** | The False Death Notice | Cael, Isolde, Nio, Order of Rainless Stone | The Ministry reports that Nio died during transfer. No body is returned. Cael and Isolde accept the notice as proof that Nio is dead. |
| **Year -8** | The Gate Collapse | Rovan, Cael, Isolde, Order of Rainless Stone | Rovan Arvane, an Elaris drainage and pressure-gate worker, traces Nio's sealed transfer route through service conduits. He dies when a maintenance gate is sealed and collapses. The Ministry records it as an accident and suppresses the cause. |
| **Year -1** | Isolde's Death | Isolde, Cael | Isolde's low-dose Brine-Spore infection remains latent for years, then causes chronic organ damage and a fatal secondary illness. She refuses Ministry treatment and dies roughly six months before Chapter 1. Cael begins living alone. |
