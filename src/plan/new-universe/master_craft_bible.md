## 1. Executive Principles & Narrative Philosophy

### 1.1 Causation over Sequence: The Law of "Therefore" and "But" (ดังนั้น / แต่ทว่า)
The fundamental defect of low-quality fiction and undisciplined language model outputs is the **"And then..." (แล้วก็...)** narrative disease. In this failed mode, scenes are arranged as a chronological checklist of events happening in linear succession without psychological inevitability:
$$\text{Event A happened} \longrightarrow \text{and then Event B happened} \longrightarrow \text{and then Event C happened}$$

In the *New Universe* narrative engine, every beat, paragraph, and chapter transition must strictly obey the **Law of Strict Causation**:
$$\text{Condition A exists} \longrightarrow \mathbf{THEREFORE}\text{ (ดังนั้น) Action B occurs} \longrightarrow \mathbf{BUT}\text{ (แต่ทว่า) Obstacle C pushes back}$$

* **The "Therefore..." Engine (Causal Escalation)**: Characters do not act randomly. A character's choice must be the inescapable logical or emotional consequence of the preceding pressure. If Kyōka chooses to remain silent during a confrontation, it is *therefore* because she has calculated that verbal defense will trigger her former peer group's vindictive rage against Ren.
* **The "But..." Engine (The Complication Mandate)**: No action succeeds smoothly. When a character executes a deliberate choice, the environment or another human agent must push back with unyielding friction. When the protagonist correctly solves Kyōka's locked-room puzzle, *but* instead of falling into his arms, Kyōka immediately draws back, recognizing that his deductive ability poses an existential threat to her carefully fortified solitude.
* **Negative Rule**: Never begin a paragraph with passive sequential markers such as *"หลังจากนั้นไม่นาน"*, *"ต่อมา"*, or *"แล้วจู่ๆ ก็มี..."* unless an external, unheralded catastrophe is deliberately deployed to shatter narrative equilibrium.

### 1.2 Psychic Distance Calibration: The Gardner 5-Level Continuum in Thai
Psychic distance (the distance between the reader's consciousness and the character's interiority, as defined by John Gardner) must be dynamically modulated rather than parked in a bland, safe middle distance.

```
[FAR / PANORAMIC] ─── Level 1 ─── Level 2 ─── Level 3 ─── Level 4 ─── Level 5 ─── [CLOSE / VISCERAL]
```

#### Level 1: Panoramic / Far (ภววิสัยมุมกว้าง — ขอบเขตเวลาและภูมิศาสตร์)
* **Function**: Used exclusively for scene establishing, time skips, season shifts, and transitions between major narrative routes.
* **Prose Texture**: Objective, detached, wide-angle cinematic lens.
* **Production Thai Standard**:
  > ย่านชานเมืองทางตะวันตกของโตเกียวในปลายเดือนพฤศจิกายนถูกปกคลุมด้วยไอหนาวแรกของฤดู ลมแห้งแล้งจากทิศเหนือพัดพาเศษใบแปะก๊วยสีเหลืองกรอบให้หมุนคว้างอยู่บนรางรถไฟสายชูโอ บ้านเรือนสองชั้นปลูกชิดติดกันเงียบสงัด ควันสีขาวจางๆ จากท่อระบายความร้อนของโรงอาบน้ำสาธารณะลอยอ้อยอิ่งตัดกับท้องฟ้าสีเทาตุ่น

#### Level 2: Objective Description (การสังเกตการณ์เชิงประจักษ์ภายนอก)
* **Function**: Establishing spatial relationships, character entrances, and physical blocking before dramatic friction begins.
* **Prose Texture**: Camera tracks motion, posture, and spatial geometry without internal judgment.
* **Production Thai Standard**:
  > ประตูบานเลื่อนไม้โอ๊กของห้องพักหมายเลข 204 เปิดแง้มไว้เพียงสองนิ้ว ด้านในปรากฏโต๊ะเตี้ยญี่ปุ่นตัวยาวที่เต็มไปด้วยกองหนังสือปกอ่อนและสมุดจดบันทึกวิจัย หญิงสาวเจ้าของห้องนั่งชันเข่าอยู่ริมหน้าต่างบานกระจก เสื้อคาร์ดิแกนไหมพรมสีเทาเข้มตัวหลวมคลุมทับเสื้อยืดคอกลมสีขาว ปลายนิ้วชี้ของเธอจรดค้างอยู่ตรงมุมล่างของหน้ากระดาษ

#### Level 3: Close Dramatic Distance (ความใกล้ชิดเชิงสถานการณ์ — มีการรับรู้ของตัวละคร)
* **Function**: Standard narrative baseline for dialogue, interpersonal tension, and kinetic interaction.
* **Prose Texture**: Descriptions are actively filtered through the POV character’s sensory preferences and emotional stakes.
* **Production Thai Standard**:
  > กลิ่นไอคั่วเข้มข้นของชาโฮจิฉะที่เริ่มเย็นชืดลอยแตะปลายจมูก เสียงเคาะปากกาลูกลื่นของเร็นดังเป็นจังหวะสะเปะสะปะอยู่ฝั่งตรงข้าม ทุกครั้งที่เจ้าน้องชายถอนหายใจยาว ไหล่ของเขาจะลู่ลงจนแทบชิดขอบโต๊ะ เคียวกะไม่ได้เงยหน้าขึ้นจากหนังสือ แต่สายตาของเธอจับสังเกตเห็นรอยหมึกสีน้ำเงินที่เปรอะเป็นปื้นบนข้อนิ้วชี้ของเขาได้อย่างแม่นยำ

#### Level 4: Free Indirect Discourse / FID (การผสานเสียงผู้เล่าเข้ากับสำนึกของตัวละคร)
* **Function**: Emotional turning points, high-stakes decisions, and internal rationalizations.
* **Prose Texture**: The boundary between narrator and character dissolves; the narrator adopts the character's exact vocabulary, doubts, and tempo without using filter tags like *"เธอคิดว่า"* or *"เขารู้สึกว่า"*.
* **Production Thai Standard**:
  > ให้มันได้อย่างนี้สิ ทำไมต้องเป็นวันนี้ด้วย พยายามจัดระเบียบทุกอย่างไว้ในมุมที่ปลอดภัยแล้วแท้ๆ แต่เด็กหนุ่มคนนี้กลับเดินดุ่มๆ ข้ามเส้นเข้ามาหน้าตาเฉย ปริศนาบนกระดาษนั่นไม่ได้ยากเกินไปหรอก ใครที่มีตาและไม่โง่เขลาจนเกินไปก็มองออกทั้งนั้น แต่สายตาที่เขามองตรงมา... แววตาที่ไม่ยอมหลบเลี่ยงนั่นต่างหากที่น่ารำคาญที่สุด

#### Level 5: Stream of Consciousness / Visceral Shock (กระแสสำนึกและวิกฤตสรีรวิทยา)
* **Function**: Reserved exclusively for physical trauma, catastrophic panic, or the shattering of emotional defenses (e.g., Kyōka's head trauma in Stage 3, the snowy breakdown in Stage 4).
* **Prose Texture**: Syntax fragments, sensory overload, autonomic breakdown, absolute raw immediacy.
* **Production Thai Standard**:
  > หูอื้อ เสียงแหลมสูงกรีดแทงทะลุแก้วหู ร้อน ของเหลวอุ่นจัดข้นคลั่กไหลอาบผ่านคิ้วขวา เข้าตา แสบ ภาพตรงหน้าแตกเป็นเสี่ยงๆ พื้นคอนกรีตเย็นเฉียบกลิ่นฝุ่นปูนกับสนิมเหล็ก ห้ามล้ม ล้มไม่ได้ ถ้าขยับสะโพกไม่ได้ข้อต่อจะหลุด ปลายเท้าซ้ายเกี่ยว ล็อก ดึง หักเสียงกระดูกลั่นเปรี๊ยะในความมืด หายใจไม่ออก เลือดคาวคลุ้งเต็มปาก

### 1.3 Sensory Grounding & Setting as Narrative Pressure
Setting is never neutral wallpaper; it is an active antagonist or psychological mirror that exerts relentless physical pressure on the characters:
1. **The Principle of Occupational Filtering**: A character does not notice "everything." What they perceive reveals their background, profession, and psychological wounds:
   * **Kyōka (ISTP / BJJ Athlete / Bug Bounty Analyst)**: Notices friction coefficients of flooring, centers of gravity, escape routes, structural weaknesses in door locks, and anomalies in a speaker's vocal micro-cadence.
   * **Ren (INFP / Sensitive Artist / Mood Maker)**: Notices shifts in room temperature, the heavy silence following a cruel remark, suppressed tears in the corner of an eye, and the defensive posture of someone pretending to be fine.
   * **Haruto (INTP / Theoretical Physicist)**: Notices thermodynamic equilibrium, the angle of winter light through glass, the steady degradation of paper fibers, and the silence required for mathematical clarity.
   * **Kaori (ISFJ / Intuitive Mother)**: Notices the chill in a guest's hands, the smell of street dust on a school uniform, the tension in a daughter's jaw, and the emotional depletion behind a forced polite smile.
2. **Environmental Interaction**: Characters must constantly interact with physical objects. Props carry emotional weight. A teacup is not just a prop; its cooling liquid measures the passage of time; a frayed shoelace indicates a hasty escape; a torn paper sleeve marks suppressed rage.

### 1.5 Sentence Rhythm, Cadence Decoupling, and Anti-Translation-ese Invariants
To achieve elite literary quality, the narrative voice must strictly adhere to three stylistic laws:
1. **Decoupling Procedural Action Chains (ตัดวงจรประโยคหุ่นยนต์)**:
   * *Banned AI Pattern*: Stacking repetitive subject-verb chains: `ฉัน + [กริยา 1] + ก่อนจะ + [กริยา 2] + พร้อมกับ + [กริยา 3]`.
   * *Mandate*: Break the sequence. Pair actions with internal evaluation, sensory resistance, or atmospheric detail.
2. **Sentence Rhythm Modulation (จังหวะความยาวประโยค)**:
   * Short, fragmentary clauses for shock, physical impacts, and kinetic combat (1–5 words).
   * Medium compound sentences for analytical deduction, tactical movement, and sharp dialogue beats (10–20 words).
   * Long, rhythmic, textured sentences for psychological interiority, atmospheric weight, and deep memory recall (25–45 words).
3. **Absolute Purge of Translation-ese Calques (กำจัดสำนวนแปลภาษาอังกฤษทื่อๆ)**:
   * ❌ Banned: *"มันไม่สำคัญว่า..."* $\longrightarrow$ ✅ Standard: *"จะอย่างไรก็ช่าง..."* หรือ *"ไม่เกี่ยวกับว่า..."*
   * ❌ Banned: *"เขาพบว่าตัวเองกำลัง..."* $\longrightarrow$ ✅ Standard: *"เขารู้ตัวอีกทีก็..."*
   * ❌ Banned: *"ในตอนท้ายของวัน..."* $\longrightarrow$ ✅ Standard: *"ท้ายที่สุดแล้ว..."*
   * ❌ Banned: *"เธอเป็นเจ้าของรอยยิ้ม..."* $\longrightarrow$ ✅ Standard: *"เธอยิ้มออกมา..."*
   * ❌ Banned: *"ไม่สามารถช่วยได้แต่ต้องรู้สึก..."* $\longrightarrow$ ✅ Standard: *"อดรู้สึกไม่ได้ว่า..."*

## 2. Anti-AI Tropes & Repetition Guards

### 2.1 Physical Trope Purge: The Ahoge (Cowlick) Silhouette Guard
* **The Root Cause Pathology**: In the raw chat transcripts (`kyoka_chat_export.json`), the single strand of hair at the crown of Kyōka's head (*ahoge* / ปอยผม) was weaponized by the AI into an animated emotional radar, twitching in 92.3% of all dialogue turns. Hair was depicted as rotating like a helicopter blade, standing vertically on end, sparking like an electrical antenna, and dropping limply in sorrow.
* **The Biological Reality**: Hair follicles contain zero skeletal muscle tissue and zero motor neurons. Hair cannot express embarrassment, shock, calculation, or romantic arousal.
* **The Immutable Ahoge Laws**:
  1. **Strict Ban on Dynamic Verbs**: The following verbs are strictly prohibited when applied to hair: `กระดิก`, `ดีดผึง`, `หมุนติ้วๆ`, `กระตุกกึกๆ`, `ลู่ลงตามอารมณ์`, `ชี้ฟ้าตามแรงดันเลือด`.
  2. **Static Silhouette Restriction**: The ahoge may *only* be mentioned as an incidental visual element of her unkempt, natural silhouette (e.g., *"เส้นผมชี้ฟูด้านบนที่ไม่เข้ารูป"*), capped at a maximum of **once per 3 to 5 chapters**.
  3. **Emotional Peak Zero Tolerance**: In moments of dramatic revelation, physical violence, medical trauma, or family catharsis, any mention of hair shape or movement is strictly forbidden.

### 2.2 Intellectual Trope Purge: Banning Equation & STEM Metaphor Hallucinations
* **The Root Cause Pathology**: When instructed that Kyōka has an IQ of 135–140 and Haruto is a theoretical physicist, the language model defaulted to lazy purple prose, forcing characters to proclaim: *"ฉันมองโลกเป็นชุดสมการ"*, *"นายคือตัวแปรที่แก้ไม่ได้"*, and *"สมองของฉันเกิดอาการ Buffer Overflow"*.
* **The High-IQ Realism Principle**: Authentic mathematical and scientific minds do not walk through domestic life narrating algebraic metaphors. Genuine high-level Ti-dominant intelligence manifests through **pragmatic spatial deduction, structural problem solving, and immediate pattern recognition**:
  * Noticing the physical tell of someone hiding an injury.
  * Calculating the arrival time of bad weather from barometric drop and air humidity without uttering the word "thermodynamics."
  * Anticipating a conversational dead-end three steps ahead and offering a quiet, practical alternative.
* **Prohibited Phrasing**:
  * ❌ *"ฉันมองคนรอบตัวเป็นตัวแปร..."*
  * ❌ *"สมการบทนี้ไม่มีคำตอบ..."*
  * ❌ *"ระบบตรรกะในสมองเกิดอาการชัตดาวน์..."*

### 2.3 Combat vs. Domestic Boundary: Purging Slapstick Chokeholds and Violent Intimacy
* **The Root Cause Pathology**: The AI degraded Kyōka’s elite Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu training into a repetitive, slapstick domestic abuse trope—threatening to choke her brother Ren with a Guillotine Choke over homework, threatening to break her husband's bones, or wrestling on the tatami mat as a romance substitute.
* **The Martial Arts Gravitas Mandate**:
  1. **Sanctity of Combat Stakes**: BJJ is a lethal, bone-breaking martial science. Kyōka’s physical capability exists solely for **Stage 3: The Alleyway Ambush**, where she uses kinetic leverage to defend herself and brutally break an attacker's leg in a life-or-death crisis.
  2. **Absolute Domestic Ban**: Kyōka is an emotionally disciplined martial artist. In daily family life, she **NEVER** applies submissions, chokeholds, or physical threats to Ren, the protagonist, or her parents.
  3. **Domestic Power Expression**: Her authority in the household is conveyed entirely through **stillness, unbroken eye contact, quiet territorial boundaries, and tactical deprivation** (e.g., smoothly sliding the teapot out of reach, unplugging a computer monitor, or turning a book page in dead silence).

### 2.4 Emotional Economy: The Single-Tear Economy & Purging Weepy Inflation
* **The Root Cause Pathology**: In the chat transcript, characters wept in 40% of all turns—crying tears of joy over pregnancy announcements, weeping into teacups, blubbering over homework results, and crying tears of embarrassment behind cushions.
* **The Single-Tear Economy for Kyōka**:
  * **The Rule of Singular Catharsis**: Takahashi Kyōka’s stoic, analytical emotional armor shatters **EXACTLY ONCE** in the entire narrative arc: **Stage 4, during the snowy family reunion in the arms of Ren, Kaori, and Haruto**.
  * **Pre-Stage 4 Iron Law**: In Stages 1, 2, and 3, Kyōka must never shed tears, have moist eyes (`น้ำตาคลอเบ้า`), or weep silently. Any draft depicting Kyōka with tears before Stage 4 is defective and must be rejected immediately.
* **Ren's Tear Governance**: Ren is an INFP mood-maker who carries quiet, mature emotional sensitivity, not a blubbering cartoon coward. He is permitted genuine tears only during genuine tragedy (e.g., when hospitalized after being beaten half to death defending his sister), and even then, he attempts to cover it with a self-deprecating smile.

### 2.5 Autonomic Physiology: Purging "สั่นระริก" (Trembling) & Clutched Fist Puppetry
* **The Ban on Mechanical Physical Tells**:
  * `สั่นระริก` (trembling/quivering) occurred 67 times in 33 turns across the chat export, applied indiscriminately to pupils (`ม่านตาสั่นระริก`), hair, fingers, voices, and entire bodies.
  * Clenching fists until knuckles turn white (`กำหมัดแน่นจนเล็บจิกเนื้อ`) was the universal shorthand for anger.
* **Authentic Autonomic Replacement**:
  * **Suppressed Sorrow / Dread**: Dry swallowing, a slow and deliberate exhalation through the nose to prevent jaw tremors, gazing fixatedly at an inanimate object (a water stain, a chipped saucer rim), or sudden unnatural immobility.
  * **Suppressed Rage**: Absolute quietness of movement, lowering the vocal register by half an octave, deliberate relaxation of shoulder blades to mask muscle tension, and unblinking, predatory focus.
  * **Embarrassment / Fluster**: Pulling a sweater collar up over the mouth, shifting weight away from the interlocutor, taking a measured sip of cold tea, or changing the physical task at hand.

## 3. Showing vs. Telling & Anti-Glazing Protocol

### 3.1 Absolute Ban on Attribute Declarations (The Zero-IQ-Mention Rule)
Telling the reader that a character is smart is an admission of authorial incompetence. Declaring that Kyōka has an "IQ of 140" or a "ตรรกะระดับโอลิมปิก" instantly destroys the illusion of reality:
* **The Zero-IQ-Mention Rule**: The terms `ไอคิว`, `IQ`, `135`, `140`, `อัจฉริยะ`, and `สมองอัจฉริยะ` are **strictly prohibited** from appearing in narrative prose, dialogue, and internal monologue.
* **The Forensic Showing Standard**: The reader must deduce the character's intellectual caliber purely through observational competence:
  1. **Speed of Physical Diagnosis**: Identifying that a visitor walked through mud because their pant cuff is rolled up unevenly, without explaining the deduction unless necessary.
  2. **Tactical Economy**: Solving a complex dispute with a single, highly leveraged action rather than an exhaustive philosophical speech.
  3. **Delayed Foresight**: Preparing an umbrella, an extra key, or a thermal flask twenty minutes before it is requested, operating on probabilities without bragging.

### 3.3 The Friction Mandate (บัญญัติสร้างแรงเสียดทาน)
No conflict in *New Universe* may resolve cleanly, quickly, or without emotional cost. Unearned narrative ease ("Frictionless Conquest") is strictly forbidden:
1. **The 3-Minute Conquest Prohibition**: No psychological barrier, trauma, or romantic conflict may be dissolved in a single conversation. In the original chat export, Kyōka opened her heart and accepted the protagonist after he solved a single riddle in three minutes. This is an insult to traumatic reality.
   * *The Mandate*: Overcoming Kyōka's defensive isolation requires **months of patient, quiet co-existence, shared silence, escalating crises, physical violence, and family intervention**.
2. **Adult Authority & Parental Resistance**:
   * **Haruto (Father)**: A fifty-year-old theoretical physicist does not grant his blessing to a high schooler because the boy "looked him in the eye." Haruto's resistance must be rooted in deep, protective paternal skepticism. He has watched his daughter suffer severe isolation; he must question whether an adolescent peer has the emotional resilience to carry the weight of Kyōka's trauma.
   * **Kaori (Mother)**: Kaori's maternal instinct is not a cheerleader squad. She worries about the repeat of past tragedies. Her blessing is earned only after observing consistent, selfless behavior under genuine adversity.
3. **Institutional and Environmental Friction**:
   * Wounds bleed; bones take six to eight weeks to knit; medical emergencies involve cold hospital hallways, mounting bills, bureaucratic consent forms, and physical exhaustion—not miracle recoveries.

In failed amateur writing, secondary characters exist solely as a "fan club" to validate the protagonist's romance and competence:
