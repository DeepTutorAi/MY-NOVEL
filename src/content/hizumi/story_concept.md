# Hizumi (歪み) — Story Concept Specification
> **Official Title**: Hizumi (歪み / ความบิดเบี้ยว)  
> **Type**: Crossover Arc (*Tsukinomi* × *Kusabi*)  
> **Version**: 2.5 (Comprehensive Integration: 5-Day Progression, Engineering Cascade, Man of Medan Dynamics, Sōsa Nika Investigation, and AI Scoping Engine)

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Pillars
* **Title & Theme**: *Hizumi* (歪み — Strain / Distortion / The Warping of Consciousness & Steel).
* **Genre**: Modern Sci-Fi + Psychological Supernatural Horror / Corporate Crime Mystery.
* **Core Inspiration / Dynamics**:
  * *Man of Medan* style psychological horror: Group paranoia, cognitive distortion, mistaking allies for threats, waking nightmares confined to an isolated vessel.
  * *Human Hubris (The "Ultron" Principle)*: Humanity creating advanced technology intended to be a revolutionary benefit, but fundamentally lacking total understanding or control over what they unleashed. Trying to build a "god" only to treat it as a tool.
* **Dual-Track Narrative Engine**:
  * **Track A (The Ship / Horror & Chaos)**: 5-day progression from glamorous normality to uncontrollable mass hallucinations and survival collapse, ending at dawn on Day 5 when the machine is plunged into the ocean.
  * **Track B (The Mainland / Sōsa Nika Investigation)**: Police procedural tracking an ostensibly unrelated corporate financial fraud that gradually unravels the dark underbelly connecting to the ship, culminating in emergency rescue coordination and truth revelation.

## 2. Integrated Timeline & Chronology (The 5-Day Arc)

```
[Day 0: Embarkation] 
   └── Night 0: Subtle onset, dismissed as fatigue/motion sickness.
[Day 1: Normality, Gala Preparations & Machine Diagnostics]
   ├── Outward luxury, reunion of peers, Nao's undercover reconnaissance.
   ├── Machine anomaly detected: The designated engineering design team spends the entire day troubleshooting.
   └── Signal begins degrading: Phone/Wi-Fi becomes intermittent and unstable (rationalized internally by ship passengers).
[Day 2: The Demonstration & The Midnight Catastrophe]
   ├── Evening: Demonstration and system overclock under management pressure.
   └── Night 2 (Late Night / 02:00–04:00 AM): Fatal breakdown at the epicenter.
       - The engineering team, closest to the core, suffers severe acute psychosis and dies in the machine room.
       - Some characters maintain their sanity.
[Day 3: Total Blackout & Discovery of the Dead]
   ├── Morning: Zero signal across all communications (caused directly by the runaway machine acting as a jamming emitter).
   ├── The corpses of the engineering team are discovered in the locked machine bay.
   └── Widespread symptoms: The machine emits continuous violent waves across a wide radius; paranoia and waking dread spread.
[Day 4: Total Pandemonium (วินาศสันตะโร)]
   ├── Collapse of social order and ship command; near-universal frenzy and violent hallucinations.
   └── Night 4 Climax: The sane survivors must execute an extreme mission to stop the broadcast.
[Before Day 5 / Pre-Dawn Climax]
   └── The Sinking: The machine is successfully cast overboard into the ocean abyss before the morning of Day 5.
[Day 5: 06:00 AM (Dawn & Aftermath)]
   ├── Sunlight breaks; the horror terminates abruptly as the radiating source is swallowed by the sea.
   └── Arrival of Coast Guard / rescue vessels coordinated by Ryoya's mainland investigation; grim physical reckoning.
```

## 3. Technology & The Inexplicable (Sci-Fi Grounding + Supernatural Dread)

### 3.1 The Technology: Grounded, Legitimate, and High-Utility
* **Design Purpose**: A revolutionary breakthrough (e.g., environmental neural stabilization, advanced closed-loop biospheric atmosphere regulation, or neuro-cognitive regenerative field for human fatigue/stress).
* **Pre-Voyage Track Record**: Thoroughly tested in controlled laboratory environments with overwhelmingly positive metrics.
* **The Fatal Blind Spot**: Corporate leadership and engineers understood minor side effects existed, but grossly underestimated how the system would behave when scaled to thousands of interacting human brains in a closed metal hull at sea.

### 3.2 The Catalyst: Human Error, Engineering Desperation & Runaway State
* **Day 1 Diagnosis**: The machine exhibits irregular harmonic fluctuations. The engineering team is ordered to troubleshoot and keep it running without shutting it down due to the high-stakes investor gala.
* **Night 2 Escalation**: Working within immediate proximity to the unshielded emitter under high stress, the engineering team suffers acute cognitive collapse, physically damaging control consoles and locking the machine into an irreversible, self-sustaining runaway loop before perishing.
* **Day 3 Direct Jamming**: The unthrottled machine turns the steel structure into a massive transmission antenna, completely destroying all external communications and drowning the vessel in continuous neuro-resonance.

### 3.3 The "Supernatural" Quality: The Inexplicable
* The horror operates under the definition of *the genuinely inexplicable*:
  * Once the cognitive filter of the collective passengers breaks, phenomena occur that defy standard chemical or neurological explanation.
  * Shared delusions where multiple people see and interact with the exact same terrifying entities or dead figures (*Man of Medan* influence).
  * Leaving the audience and characters to confront the unsettling question: *Did the machine merely cause a hallucination, or did it strip away the veil between reality and something that should never have returned?*

### 4.1 Timeline Alignment & Shared Universe Context
* **In-Universe Timing**: Set roughly 1 year after the 2018 epilogue of *Tsukinomi* (circa **2019–2020**).
* **Cross-Series Age Lock**:
  * **Takahashi Hina & New Protagonist**: **Age 23** (4th-year university students).
  * **Amamiya Nao**: **Age 28** (Investigative journalist).
  * **Arai Ryoya**: **Age 28** (Police investigator with 5–6 years on the force, transferred into Sōsa Nika 1–2 years ago).
  * **Takahashi Haruto (Reference / Extra 2 appearance)**: **Age 29** (Completing/holding his physics doctorate in Tokyo).

### 4.2 Ship Cast (4 Core Characters)
* **New Protagonist (Female, Age 23)**:
  * A 4th-year university student in the same faculty as Hina, currently struggling because she has not yet secured an internship or job offer.
  * Attends the luxury voyage via an invitation from her father, a technician working for the host corporation on the ship, while she is still searching for her direction.
  * **Progressive Hallucination Arc (Father & Constructed Fear)**:
    1. *Early Phase*: Interacts with her real, living father, who tells her something that lingers in her mind.
    2. *Mid-to-Late Phase (Constructed Father Hallucination)*: After her father dies on Night 2 (unknown to her and the reader), her mind extrapolates from his earlier words and constructs a realistic hallucination of him. Because no other characters are present during their conversations, the illusion holds until late in the story when someone notices and asks *who* she is talking to.
    3. *Night 4 Breakdown*: Upon learning the truth that her father is already dead, her devastated mental state combines with the machine's peak distortion. Like a terrified child whose mind constructs a warped manifestation of their deepest fears, her perception twists into a monstrous delusion, causing her to violently attack Hina and nearly kill her before Nao pulls her back to reality.
* **Takahashi Hina (Age 23)**:
  * 4th-year university student in the same faculty as the protagonist; recommended to work aboard the vessel as a student intern handling event booths, guest reception, and MC duties for the gala.
  * Reunites with the protagonist aboard the ship. Brings her grounded perception and intuitive sensitivity to bear as the collective delusion deepens, though she initially holds little respect for Nao's frivolous persona.
  * Nearly loses her life during the protagonist's psychotic break on Night 4.
* **Amamiya Nao (Age 28)**:
  * Investigative journalist aboard the ship undercover.
  * **Concealed Identity & Comedic Notebook Drop**: In the early chapters, his real name is hidden from the reader (using an alias or remaining unnamed in the protagonist's POV). While putting on a handsome, womanizing "playboy" act, he clumsily and accidentally drops a crucial notebook/notes containing codes and records. The protagonist picks it up to return it, but he vanishes in the commotion.
  * **The Unnamed Phone Contact**: Early on, he is overheard speaking on the phone to an "unnamed contact" (who is actually Ryoya, revealed later from Ryoya's perspective) admitting he lost his notebook, serving as a subtle Easter egg before his true identity is unveiled.
  * **Late-Game MVP & Redemption**: Steps up in the climax to stop the protagonist from killing Hina and snaps her back to sanity, redeeming his past trauma from *Kusabi*.
* **Protagonist's Father**:
  * Corporate employee/technician stationed on the vessel. Appears as his real self early on before perishing on Night 2, subsequently persisting as a constructed hallucination in the protagonist's mind.
* **The Engineering Design Team & Ship Ensemble**:
  * The specialized technical personnel who perish in the locked machine room in late Night 2, plus the large-scale cast of VIPs, scientists, and crew.

### 4.3 Mainland Cast: Second Investigation Division (捜査二課 — 4 to 5 Member Team)
* **Arai Ryoya (Age 28 — Mainland POV)**:
  * Detective in Tokyo Metropolitan Police Dept, having served 5–6 years as an officer and transferred into Sōsa Nika only 1–2 years prior.
  * Exhibits sharp observational skills and analytical brilliance, but still lacks experience in the specialized complexities of white-collar corporate evidence gathering, requiring true teamwork rather than solo heroics.
* **Sōsa Nika Team Members (Fleshed-out Supporting Cast)**:
  * **Veteran Senior Investigator (Male, Age 35–40+)**: Experienced specialist in financial and corporate crime who guides the procedural direction and covers Ryoya's blind spots.
  * **Senior Investigator / Interrogator (Male, Age 30–31)**: Only 2–3 years older than Ryoya; handles witness interrogation and field coordination.
  * **Female Investigator (Female, Age 28)**: Same age/cohort as Ryoya; works closely alongside him analyzing records, compliance trails, and evidence.
  * **Unit Supervisor / Section Chief**: Assigns the initial routine corporate audit (Case A) to the team.
* **Ryoya's Supervisor / Partner**:
  * Senior officer who initially assigns Ryoya a seemingly mundane financial irregularity or embezzlement case at an unrelated affiliate.
* **The Investigation Progression (Indirect Route)**:
  * **Stage 1 (Mundane Inception)**: Routine financial audit into a third-party commercial firm.
  * **Stage 2 (The Hidden Link)**: Shell companies trace back to the parent conglomerate operating the luxury research vessel. Uncovers sealed historical settlements and buried rumors of past experimental anomalies.
  * **Stage 3 (The Race against Silence)**: As the ship's communications go dark on Day 3 and 4, Ryoya's paper trail exposes the catastrophic vulnerability of the tech aboard, prompting an urgent Coast Guard/rescue dispatch.

## 5. Narrative Dynamics: Two Halves of a Single Truth
* **The Reader's Experience**:
  * On the ship: Visceral terror, visceral confusion, emotional survival, subjective distortion of reality.
  * On the mainland: Objective logic, forensic paper trails, deciphering *why* the ship's inhabitants are seeing what they see.
* **Resolution (Dawn, Day 5)**:
  * At 06:00 AM, morning sunlight hits the drifting vessel.
  * The machine has sunk into the deep sea; the resonance fades; hallucinations stop.
  * The survivors are left amid the physical wreckage of a self-inflicted disaster, facing the rescue teams dispatched through Ryoya's mainland discovery.

### 5.1 Post-Story Extras (3-Part Epilogue / Bonus Structure)
* **Extra 1: *Kusabi* Reunion & Present-Day Lives (Short)**
  * A reunion gathering of the five *Kusabi* survivors (**Ryoya, Nao, Risa, Kaito, and Misaki**) updating where each character is in their life now.
  * During their conversation, Ryoya excuses himself early because he promised his pregnant wife he wouldn't stay out late—naturally revealing that he is now married and expecting a child.
* **Extra 2: Hina's Perspective, The Kaori Easter Egg & Haruto's Hospital Visit (Medium — slightly longer than Extra 1)**
  * Recounts the crisis on the ship from **Hina's POV**, showing the hallucinations she experienced—including a subtle, ambiguous appearance of **Kaori** helping her (leaving the reader to question whether it was a hallucination born from Hina's own mind/sketches or Kaori's spirit, knowing she had already passed on).
  * Concludes at the hospital shortly after Hina wakes up, where **Haruto (Age 29)** arrives to visit her. Rather than recounting her dreams or over-connecting the vision to him, Hina makes a fleeting, lighthearted remark/joke before chatting normally with her brother, keeping the experience feeling like her own quiet internal thought.
* **Extra 3: The Protagonist's Recovery & Moving Forward**
  * Told from the **Protagonist's POV**: having sustained lighter injuries, she is discharged from the hospital before Hina and comes to visit Hina's room.
  * Provides emotional resolution and closure for her lingering guilt and grief over her father, allowing her character to finally move forward with her life.

## 6. AI Creative Scoping Engine (Open Inquiry Vectors)

This section serves as the prompt engine for AI co-writers and collaborators. When generating scenes, outlines, or prose, the AI must explore creative answers within these author-defined inquiry vectors:

### Vector 1: Symptom Asymmetry & Grounding Countermeasures
* **Question**: Why do certain individuals (like the engineers) collapse rapidly while others retain sanity through Day 3 and 4? What specific conditions modulate vulnerability?
* **Question**: What believable, in-universe grounding actions or physical countermeasures can sane survivors discover to temporarily clear their perception and function?

### Vector 2: Ship-Side Rationalization on Days 1 and 2
* **Question**: How do people on the ship (passengers, officers, crew) naturally explain away the degrading communications and early sensory anomalies on Day 1 without suspecting a supernatural/technological catastrophe?
* **Question**: What social dynamics and corporate assurances prevent immediate panic during the initial phase?

### Vector 3: The Runaway Mechanism & Electromagnetic Jamming
* **Question**: What exact sequence of events during the engineers' attempt to fix the machine in Night 2 leads to the controls being destroyed and the system locked into permanent, wide-radius radiation?
* **Question**: How does this emission directly eliminate all satellite, cellular, and maritime radio communications by Day 3?

### Vector 4: The Mechanics of the Sinking
* **Question**: What is the physical layout and mechanism on the ship (e.g., modular research bay, cargo hatch, stern ramp, or moon pool) that allows the multi-ton machine to be jettisoned into the ocean?
* **Question**: What specific physical and human obstacles do the surviving characters face to reach the release mechanism during the pandemonium of Night 4?

### Vector 5: Sōsa Nika's Breakthrough
* **Question**: What concrete document, ledger, or testimony provides Ryoya with the breakthrough realization that the ship is in mortal danger, justifying an emergency Coast Guard dispatch?
