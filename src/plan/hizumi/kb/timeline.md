# Integrated 5-Day Timeline: Hizumi (歪み)

**Canon Year**: 2019–2020

## Day 1: Embarkation, Gala & Early Encounters
- **Track A (Ship)**:
  - Luxury technology launch gala for Project "AETHEL Core".
  - Shiraishi Reika boards with her father, technician Shiraishi Shinji, who gives her quiet encouragement and warns her to head toward the aft deck if the ship's hum ever sounds wrong.
  - Hina and her classmate Saeki Koharu work the exhibition booth under the toxic supervision of Yamasaki Ryota (with a natural, passing mention of Hina's brother). Koharu bridges the reunion between Hina and Reika.
  - Amamiya Nao infiltrates under a playboy facade; drops a coded notebook near Reika and vanishes before she can return it. Reika overhears Nao on a mysterious phone call (with Ryoya) mentioning the lost notebook.
- **Track B (Mainland — Sōsa Nika)**:
  - Supervisor Saruwatari assigns Ryoya's team a routine accounting/tax audit on an ostensibly minor medical logistics subsidiary.
  - Ryoya and Nao exchange preliminary corporate and hardware leads via phone on Days 1–2, neither yet aware of the machine's lethal scale.

## Day 2: Early Signal Wobble & First Technician Exposure
- **Track A (Ship)**:
  - **Noon–Evening**: Satellite and radio signals experience minor instability (choppy connections observed by Captain Hayasaka, rationalized as weather).
  - **Machine Bay**: Ota Yuichi, who stays closest to the AETHEL Core the longest, is the first to suffer acute neurological symptoms (headaches, delirium, disorientation). Returning coworkers mistake this for severe illness and take him to the infirmary/rest quarters, inadvertently saving him from the Day 3 bay collapse.
  - Hina sees through Nao's flirtatious mask during booth/gala interactions.
- **Track B (Mainland)**:
  - Kawazura Eri and Takeda Masayoshi flag anomalous purchases of military-grade supercapacitors and resonance hardware disguised as medical depreciation.

## Day 3: Midday Blackout, Unseen Deaths & Storm Onset
- **Track B → Track A (Midday/Noon)**:
  - During a midday phone check-in between Ryoya and Nao, the signal degrades rapidly and cuts off mid-call (0% external communication thereafter), leaving Nao confused and alerting Ryoya that the ship has gone abnormally silent.
- **Track A (Ship — Afternoon to Night)**:
  - **Daytime**: Isolated panic-driven passenger killings begin (victims hallucinate threats and strike out in perceived self-defense). Captain Hayasaka and First Officer Nakamura quietly detain the perpetrators and suppress the incidents to prevent mass panic; Nao inadvertently witnesses or learns of this cover-up.
  - **Night 3**: Remaining engineering staff succumb to acute psychosis and perish inside the sealed machine bay, leaving the AETHEL Core completely unmanned. Separately, Shiraishi Shinji dies alone in a secluded Deck 2 service corridor breaker alcove with no witnesses. From late Day 3 onward, Reika begins hallucinating her father beside her.
  - **Weather**: A severe offshore squall, heavy sea fog, and rough swells roll in from Night 3 through Night 4, hampering maritime navigation and grounding helicopters.
- **Track B (Mainland)**:
  - Following the dropped midday call and the ship's AIS disappearance, Ryoya's team uncovers shell-company cargo leases and seizes corporate servers containing suppressed Day 2 choppy transmission logs alongside a 72-hour lethal brain-necrosis test report.

## Day 4: The "First Spark", Fires & Night 4 Pandemonium
- **Track B (Mainland)**:
  - Ryoya's team locates and pressures a co-researcher/whistleblower who confesses that similar resonance incidents occurred in prior controlled tests, that the company covered up the danger, and that the shipboard unit has no remote kill-switch.
  - Saruwatari initially blocks an emergency Coast Guard request due to lack of confirmed shipboard status and severe storm risk. After Matsuoka, Takeda, Eri, and Ryoya prove the ship is dark and drifting unpiloted past the 72-hour threshold, JCG dispatch is authorized—but delayed by inter-agency bureaucracy and the storm.
- **Track A (Ship — Evening/Dusk)**:
  - **The First Spark**: During a tense Grand Atrium confrontation, an acutely psychotic VIP stabs a crew member and leaps from the 4th-tier balcony onto the marble floor, triggering mass panic and an overnight hell.
- **Track A (Ship — Night 4 Group Split & Convergence)**:
  1. **Group 1 (Hina's Escort Group)**: Hina, Saeki Koharu, toxic senior Yamasaki Ryota, and lost 8-year-old boy Morita Sota. Yamasaki shoves another person to distract an attacker, then locks a crew safe-room door in their faces before later wandering into Ota Yuichi's blind spot and dying.
  2. **Group 2 (Reika's Group)**: Reika, Medical Assistant Takai Keisuke, and Sota's parents (Morita Shuji & Kanae). Takai observes Reika whispering to empty space and asks who she is talking to.
  3. **Group 3 (Command Group)**: Nao, Captain Hayasaka, and First Officer Nakamura. Captain Hayasaka dies in an accident during the chaos. On the bridge, Nao cross-references his Day 1–2 notes with ship power telemetry and blueprints, deducing the core is using the steel hull as a resonator. Nao instructs Nakamura to take the detachable **Portable EPIRB** to the lifeboats while Nao heads down to Deck 2.
  4. **Medical Bay Rendezvous**: Amid spreading fires and explosions, Hina's group collides with Reika's group. Sota reunites with his parents (Shuji is injured). They fall back to the Medical Bay where **Dr. Murakami** is holding a safe zone with sane survivors and tending to a symptomatic patient. Takai distributes industrial ear protection (partial acoustic mitigation) and stays with Dr. Murakami, Koharu, and the Morita family, while Nao leads Reika and Hina down toward Deck 2.
- **Track A (Ship — Night 4 Climax at Deck 2 Aft Service Corridor)**:
  - Fires, smoke, and damage render shutting down or jettisoning the AETHEL Core physically impossible.
  - Nao scouts ahead to check the hatch/route while Hina watches him. Behind them, Reika hears a hallucinated voice of her father and opens a service alcove, discovering Shinji's Day 3 corpse.
  - Reika's sanity shatters; she attacks Hina with an emergency heavy-duty industrial flashlight, nearly killing her before Nao rushes back, restrains Reika, and pulls her back to reality.
  - Ota Yuichi ambushes them, injuring Reika and cornering both Nao and Reika. Despite critical wounds, Hina forces herself up and incapacitates Ota while his guard is down.
  - Seawater floods into Deck 2, partially dampening the hull's harmonic resonance (easing symptoms slightly for survivors, though heavily affected individuals remain deranged). Nao, Reika, and Hina scramble upward through smoke and listing corridors to join Nakamura, Murakami, Takai, Koharu, and the Morita family in launching the lifeboats.

## Day 5: Sinking, Open-Sea Wait & Rescue
- **Pre-Dawn to 06:00 AM**: Survivors row and drift away in lifeboats as the cruise liner lists and sinks permanently into the ocean, taking the AETHEL Core to the abyss. Once clear of the hull's jamming radius, Nakamura's Portable EPIRB transmits the lifeboats' coordinates.
- **06:00 AM – ~10:00 AM (3–4 Hour Wait)**: As the storm breaks at dawn, survivors wait 3–4 hours on the open sea until the JCG cutter and helicopters mobilized by Ryoya's investigation reach them.

## Post-Story Extras (Chronological Order)

2. **Extra 2 (Discharge → ~2 Months Post-Incident)**: Reika's POV; discharged earlier, she says goodbye to Hina at the hospital, then cuts to ~2 months later as she works through ongoing grief and trauma with **Psychiatrist Dr. An**.
3. **Extra 3 (~4 Months Post-Incident)**: *Kusabi* 5-member reunion (Ryoya, Nao, Risa, Kaito, Misaki); Ryoya leaves early for his pregnant wife.
