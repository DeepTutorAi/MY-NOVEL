# Cast Biography: Hizumi (歪み)

## 1. Track A: Cruise Ship Main Cast

### 1.1 Shiraishi Reika (白石 麗華 / formerly Mao) — Primary Ship POV
- **Age / Role**: 23, Female, 4th-year University Student (Faculty of International Communication & Media Studies; same faculty as Hina).
- **Circumstances**: Struggling with job-hunting and feeling directionless; invited aboard by her father, a company technician stationed on the vessel.
- **8-Dimension Character Engine**:
  1. **Want**: Secure stable employment before graduation so her father no longer has to overwork himself.
  2. **Need**: Release the guilt of feeling like a burden and accept that life cannot be rigidly controlled.
  3. **Fear**: Being unwanted and left completely alone if she loses her only family pillar.
  4. **Wound**: Her mother abandoned them during childhood, leaving her blue-collar father to raise her alone.
  5. **Mask**: Forced positivity and stoicism; hides exhaustion and anxiety behind a compliant demeanor.
  6. **Contradiction**: Desires closeness with her father yet becomes curt or defensive in his presence out of fear of showing vulnerability.
  7. **Agency**: Refuses to hide passively when the ship deteriorates; descends toward the lower decks with Nao and Hina, placing her in the Deck 2 service corridor.
  8. **Breaking Point**: Hearing her father's hallucinated voice in Deck 2 and discovering his Day 3 corpse in a service alcove, shattering her sanity and triggering a violent psychotic break against Hina.

### 1.2 Takahashi Hina (高橋 陽菜)
- **Age / Role**: 23, Female, 4th-year University Student (same faculty as Reika). Working as a booth display and welcome MC intern aboard the ship.
- **Background**: Younger sister of Takahashi Haruto (*Tsukinomi*). Possesses heightened intuitive sensitivity to unnatural anomalies.
- **Arc**: Tolerates workplace bullying from her toxic senior (Yamasaki) during Days 1–2. Sees through Nao's playboy facade early on, forming a subtle, natural rapport with him. Protects a lost 8-year-old boy (Sota) during Night 4 chaos. Survives Reika's near-fatal assault in the Deck 2 corridor, then forces herself up despite severe injuries to incapacitate Ota Yuichi when he corners Nao and Reika.

### 1.3 Amamiya Nao (アマミヤ ナオ / 天宮 直)
- **Age / Role**: 28, Male, Investigative Journalist.
- **Background**: *Kusabi* survivor. Infiltrated the cruise using a "handsome, flirtatious playboy" persona to deflect suspicion while investigating corporate malpractice.
- **Key Beats**:
  - Early chapters: Appears under an alias/unnamed in Reika's POV; drops a coded notebook in a comedic encounter and vanishes before she can return it. Overheard by Reika on a mysterious phone call (with Ryoya) mentioning the lost notebook. Exchanges early leads with Ryoya on Days 1–2 before their Day 3 midday call drops out.
  - Night 4: Witnesses/uncovers the Day 3 cover-up, then coordinates with First Officer Nakamura on the bridge to match corporate hardware notes against ship power telemetry and blueprints. Instructs Nakamura to take the portable EPIRB to the lifeboats, then leads Reika and Hina to Deck 2. Scouts ahead at the critical moment, rushes back to stop Reika's assault on Hina, and escapes with both women as the ship begins to sink.

### 1.4 Shiraishi Shinji (白石 慎二)
- **Age / Role**: 51, Male, Senior Sub-Electrical & Environmental Systems Technician (Reika's father).
- **Arc**: Appears alive on Days 1–2, offering quiet encouragement and a warning about the ship's unnatural hum and heading toward the aft deck if anything goes wrong. Dies alone in a Deck 2 service corridor breaker alcove on Day 3 without anyone witnessing his death, becoming the unwitting anchor for Reika's subsequent hallucinations.

## 2. Track A: Cruise Ship Supporting Cast

### 2.1 Event & Booth Group
- **Saeki Koharu (佐伯 小春)** — Age 23, Female. Hina's classmate and fellow intern at the exhibition booth. Serves as the natural bridge introducing Reika and Hina's shared faculty background (and gives Hina a natural opening for a passing mention of her brother). Travels in Hina's survival group on Night 4, retreats to the Medical Bay safe zone, and **survives**.
- **Yamasaki Ryota (山崎 亮太)** — Age 27, Male. Toxic senior employee supervising the event booth. Consistently bullies Hina and dumps his workload onto her during Days 1–2. During Night 4, exhibits severe selfishness in two stages: first shoving/using a group member as bait against an attacker to slip away, then locking a crew safe-room door in the faces of Hina, Koharu, and Sota. **Dies** later that night after wandering into Ota Yuichi's blind spot.

### 2.2 Engineering & Threat
- **Ota Yuichi (太田 雄一)** — Age 28, Male. Senior technician working near the AETHEL Core. Suffers severe acute exposure symptoms first on Day 2 from prolonged proximity; coworkers mistake it for illness and take him to rest/infirmary, saving him from the Day 3 engineering collapse. By Days 3–4, the resonance warps his mind into a cold, calculating, intelligent killer who uses maintenance corridors and blind spots to stalk victims. **Incapacitated/defeated** by an injured Hina during the Night 4 climax after he injures Reika and corners both Nao and Reika.

### 2.3 Bridge Officers
- **Captain Hayasaka Kenji (早坂 健二)** — Age 56, Male. Ship Captain. Attempts to maintain passenger calm on Day 3 by quietly detaining early psychotic attackers and suppressing news of deaths while ordering a discreet radio call. **Dies** in an accident during the Night 4 chaos.
- **First Officer Nakamura Sosuke (中村 宗介)** — Age 36, Male. Unaware of the corporate machine's true nature initially; learns the warning/truth on Day 3 as the ship tries to radio out while communications fail. Assumes bridge responsibility after the Captain's death on Night 4, coordinates with Nao, and carries the **Portable EPIRB** into the lifeboats so it can transmit once clear of the sinking hull's jamming radius. **Survives** as a key official witness for Sōsa Nika.

### 2.4 Medical Staff
- **Dr. Murakami Hiroshi (村上 博)** — Age 48, Male. Senior Ship Physician. Handles clinical diagnoses, prescriptions, and triage during Days 1–3. On Night 4, holds down the **Medical Bay** safe zone with sane survivors while trying to treat/contain a symptomatic patient, later sheltering Takai, Koharu, and the Morita family until evacuation. **Survives**.
- **Takai Keisuke (高井 啓介)** — Age 27, Male. Medical Assistant aboard the ship. Travels with **Reika's group** on Night 4, observing Reika's dissociative symptoms firsthand (whispering to empty space). After reuniting with Hina's group and Dr. Murakami, provides industrial ear protection (partial acoustic mitigation) to Nao's group and remains at the Medical Bay to care for the injured Morita Shuji and protect the civilians. **Survives**.

### 2.5 Ordinary Passengers (The Morita Family)
- **Morita Shuji (森田 修二)** — Age 39, Male (Office worker). Injured during the Night 4 chaos; treated by Takai and Dr. Murakami.
- **Morita Kanae (森田 香苗)** — Age 36, Female (Homemaker).
- **Morita Sota (森田 颯太)** — Age 8, Male (Son).
- **Dynamic**: Ordinary vacationers with zero connection to the corporate conspiracy. During the Night 4 panic, Sota becomes separated from his parents and is protected by **Hina and Koharu** (Group 1), while Shuji and Kanae search alongside **Reika and Takai** (Group 2). The family reunites when the groups cross paths and retreat to Dr. Murakami's Medical Bay before evacuating via lifeboats.

## 3. Track B & Post-Story Mainland Cast

### 3.1 Sōsa Nika Core Investigation Team
1. **Arai Ryoya (新井 良太 / 諒也)** — Age 28, Male, Primary Mainland POV.
   - *Kusabi* survivor. Police officer for 5–6 years; transferred to Sōsa Nika (economic/financial crimes) 1–2 years ago.
   - Calm, methodical, rule-abiding, highly observant. Shares early corporate/hardware leads with Nao on Days 1–2 before their Day 3 midday call cuts off. Relies on team synergy rather than lone-wolf heroics.
2. **Saruwatari Yasuhiro (猿渡 泰宏)** — Age ~48–50, Male, Strict Supervisor / Unit Chief.
   - Strict, procedural, and frequently challenges Ryoya's hypotheses when hard evidence is lacking. Not an antagonist; acts as an institutional wall of logic requiring concrete proof before authorizing Coast Guard mobilization. Old acquaintance of Takeda.
3. **Takeda Masayoshi (武田 正義)** — Age 46, Male, Veteran Detective.
   - Close in age and long-time acquaintance of Saruwatari. Humorous and laid-back casually, but sharp, serious, and classic old-school detective when working a case. Helps balance Saruwatari's rigidity and provides tonal relief alongside Eri.
4. **Matsuoka Kengo (松岡 健吾)** — Age 31, Male, Senior Investigator.
   - Calm, experienced (below Takeda's seniority), straightforward, and direct. Handles field pressure and corporate/researcher interrogations cleanly.
5. **Kawazura Eri (川面 恵理)** — Age 28, Female, Junior Investigator.
   - High-energy newcomer bursting with drive and initiative, serving as an energetic foil to Ryoya's composed, by-the-book temperament.
6. **Co-Researcher / Whistleblower (Unnamed/Supporting)** — Developer who worked on the AETHEL Core project; breaks under police pressure/conscience on Day 4, confessing that prior suppressed incidents occurred under controlled conditions and that no remote kill-switch exists.

### 3.2 Post-Story Supporting Cast
- **Dr. An (หมออัน)** — Land-based Psychiatrist / Trauma Specialist appearing in **Extra 2 (~2 months post-incident)**, providing ongoing clinical counseling to Reika as she processes grief and trauma rather than forcing an unrealistic solo recovery.
