# Chapter Outlines & Voice Matrix: Hizumi (歪み)

**Structure**: 12 Main Chapters + 3 Chronological Extras  
**Estimated Total Scale**: ~10,600–12,400 lines (Main) + ~1,250–1,700 lines (Extras)

---

## 1. Chapter-by-Chapter Breakdown

### Act I: Setup & Early Cracks (Days 1–2)

- **Chapter 1 [Track A — Day 1]**: Embarkation & The Gala *(Est. 750–900 lines)*
  - **POV**: Shiraishi Reika
  - **Beats**:
    - Reika boards the luxury cruise liner with her father, Shiraishi Shinji, carrying quiet anxiety over her stalled job-hunting. Shinji offers quiet encouragement and warns her to head toward the aft deck if the ship's hum ever sounds wrong.
    - Exhibition booth introduction: Saeki Koharu bridges the reunion between Reika and Takahashi Hina (same university faculty). Yamasaki Ryota's toxic treatment of Hina is established; Hina makes a natural, passing mention of her brother during conversation with Koharu.
    - Amamiya Nao (unnamed/alias under a playboy facade) accidentally drops a coded notebook near Reika and vanishes before she can return it. Reika later overhears him on a mysterious phone call (with Ryoya) mentioning the lost notebook.

- **Chapter 2 [Track B — Day 1]**: Sōsa Nika & The Routine Audit *(Est. 650–800 lines)*
  - **POV**: Arai Ryoya
  - **Beats**:
    - Introduction of the 5-member Sōsa Nika unit: Unit Chief Saruwatari Yasuhiro assigns a seemingly mundane subsidiary accounting/tax audit to Ryoya, Veteran Takeda Masayoshi, Senior Investigator Matsuoka Kengo, and Junior Investigator Kawazura Eri.
    - Lighthearted team banter (Takeda's casual humor and Eri's high energy balancing Ryoya's by-the-book composure) sets a grounded, non-monotonous tone.
    - Reveals Ryoya as Nao's phone contact; the two exchange preliminary corporate and hardware leads without yet suspecting any lethal danger aboard the ship.

- **Chapter 3 [Track A + Track B — Day 2]**: Signal Wobble & First Exposure *(Est. 800–950 lines)*
  - **POV**: Reika / Hina / Ryoya (Intercut)
  - **Beats**:
    - **Track A**: Captain Hayasaka notes minor satellite/radio instability (rationalized as offshore weather). Inside the Aft Marine Research Bay, Ota Yuichi—having spent the longest shifts near the AETHEL Core—is the first to suffer severe neurological symptoms (migraines, delirium). Returning coworkers mistake this for illness and take him to rest/infirmary, saving him from the Day 3 bay collapse.
    - **Track A (Character Chemistry)**: Hina sees through Nao's flirtatious mask during booth/gala interactions, creating grounded banter and mutual observation between them.
    - **Track B**: Eri and Takeda flag anomalous purchases of military-grade supercapacitors and resonance hardware disguised as medical depreciation; Ryoya shares initial hardware context with Nao.

---

### Act II: Escalation, Unseen Deaths & Blackout (Day 3)

- **Chapter 4 [Track B → Track A — Day 3 Noon]**: Dead Air *(Est. 800–950 lines)*
  - **POV**: Ryoya → Nao
  - **Beats**:
    - **Track B**: Ryoya's team traces shell-company cargo leases directly to the cruise liner.
    - **Midday Blackout**: During a noon phone check-in between Ryoya and Nao, the connection degrades into static and severs completely mid-call (0% external communication thereafter). Nao is left confused by his dead phone, while Ryoya realizes the ship's silence is abnormal.

- **Chapter 5 [Track A — Day 3 Afternoon to Night]**: Unseen Casualties *(Est. 850–1,000 lines)*
  - **POV**: Reika / Nao
  - **Beats**:
    - Isolated panic-driven passenger killings begin (victims hallucinate threats and strike out in perceived self-defense). Captain Hayasaka and First Officer Nakamura quietly detain perpetrators and suppress the incidents to avoid mass panic; Nao inadvertently witnesses or discovers this cover-up.
    - **Night 3**: Remaining engineering staff succumb to acute psychosis and perish inside the locked machine bay, leaving the AETHEL Core unmanned. Separately, Shiraishi Shinji dies alone in a secluded Deck 2 service corridor breaker alcove. A severe offshore squall and sea fog roll in.

---

### Act III: The "First Spark" & Night 4 Pandemonium (Day 4)

- **Chapter 6 [Track B — Day 4 Morning to Afternoon]**: The 72-Hour Clock *(Est. 800–950 lines)*
  - **POV**: Ryoya
  - **Beats**:
    - Following the AIS disappearance, Sōsa Nika seizes corporate servers (uncovering suppressed Day 2 choppy transmission logs and a lethal 72-hour brain-necrosis test report) and pressures a co-researcher/whistleblower into confessing that prior suppressed incidents occurred under controlled conditions and that no remote kill-switch exists.
    - Saruwatari initially blocks an emergency Coast Guard request due to lack of shipboard proof and storm risk. Ryoya, Matsuoka, Takeda, and Eri prove the ship is dark and drifting unpiloted past the 72-hour mark, securing JCG dispatch authorization—though bureaucracy and storm conditions delay immediate arrival.

- **Chapter 7 [Track A — Day 4 Evening]**: The First Spark *(Est. 950–1,150 lines)*
  - **POV**: Reika / Hina
  - **Beats**:
    - Reika has been conversing with a hallucinated projection of her father since late Day 3; Medical Assistant Takai Keisuke (or Morita Kanae) observes her whispering to empty space and asks who she is talking to.
    - **First Spark**: In the Grand Atrium at dusk, an acutely psychotic VIP stabs a crew member and leaps from the 4th-tier balcony onto the marble floor, triggering mass panic and an overnight hell.
    - Survivors fracture into 3 groups (Hina + Koharu + Yamasaki + Sota; Reika + Takai + Morita Shuji & Kanae; Nao + Captain Hayasaka + Nakamura) plus lone hunter Ota Yuichi.

- **Chapter 8 [Track A — Night 4 Early]**: Betrayal & Bridge Deduction *(Est. 950–1,150 lines)*
  - **POV**: Hina / Nao
  - **Beats**:
    - Yamasaki Ryota exhibits two-stage selfishness—shoving another person as distraction to flee, then locking a crew safe-room door in the faces of Hina, Koharu, and Sota—before later wandering into Ota Yuichi's blind spot and dying.
    - Captain Hayasaka dies in an accident during the bridge/corridor chaos. On the bridge, Nao cross-references his Day 1–2 notes with ship power telemetry and blueprints, deducing the core is turning the steel hull into a giant resonator. Nao instructs Nakamura to take the detachable **Portable EPIRB** to the lifeboats while Nao heads below to Deck 2.

- **Chapter 9 [Track A + Track B Beat — Night 4 Mid]**: Medical Bay Rendezvous *(Est. 950–1,150 lines)*
  - **POV**: Reika / Hina / Ryoya (Brief Intercut)
  - **Beats**:
    - **Track A**: Spreading fires and explosions close off deck routes. Hina's group collides with Reika's group; Sota reunites with his parents (Shuji is injured). They fall back to the Medical Bay where Dr. Murakami is holding a safe zone with sane survivors and managing a symptomatic patient. Takai distributes industrial ear protection (partial acoustic mitigation) and remains with Murakami, Koharu, and the Morita family, while Nao meets up with Reika and Hina to lead them toward Deck 2.
    - **Track B Beat**: Brief cut to Ryoya coordinating with the storm-delayed JCG vessel past the 72-hour mark, staring at an empty radar screen.

---

### Act IV: Climax, Sinking & Dawn Rescue (Night 4 Late → Day 5)

- **Chapter 10 [Track A + Track B Beat — Night 4 Climax]**: Deck 2 Breakdown *(Est. 1,000–1,200 lines)*
  - **POV**: Reika / Hina / Ryoya (Brief Intercut)
  - **Beats**:
    - **Track A**: Smoke, fire, and structural damage in Deck 2 make shutting down or jettisoning the AETHEL Core impossible. Nao scouts ahead to check the forward hatch/route while Hina watches him.
    - Behind them, Reika hears a hallucinated voice of her father and opens a service alcove, discovering Shinji's Day 3 corpse. Her sanity shatters; she attacks Hina with an emergency industrial flashlight, nearly killing her before Nao rushes back, restrains Reika, and pulls her back to reality.
    - **Track B Beat**: Brief cut to JCG pushing through the outer squall as dawn approaches.

- **Chapter 11 [Track A — Night 4 Escape]**: Ota's Ambush & Abandon Ship *(Est. 1,000–1,200 lines)*
  - **POV**: Hina / Reika / Nao
  - **Beats**:
    - Ota Yuichi ambushes them in the Deck 2 corridor, injuring Reika and cornering both Nao and Reika. Despite critical wounds, Hina forces herself up and incapacitates Ota while his guard is down.
    - Seawater floods into Deck 2, partially dampening the hull's harmonic resonance (easing symptoms slightly for survivors, though heavily affected individuals remain deranged). Nao, Reika, and Hina scramble upward through smoke and listing corridors to join Nakamura, Murakami, Takai, Koharu, and the Morita family in launching the lifeboats.

- **Chapter 12 [Track A + Track B — Day 5 Dawn to ~10:00 AM]**: Sinking & Rescue *(Est. 850–1,000 lines)*
  - **POV**: Reika / Nao / Ryoya
  - **Beats**:
    - Survivors row and drift away from the cruise liner as it lists and sinks permanently into the ocean, taking the AETHEL Core down with it. Once clear of the hull's jamming radius, Nakamura's Portable EPIRB transmits cleanly.
    - Survivors wait 3–4 hours on the open sea after dawn (~06:00 AM to ~10:00 AM) as the storm clears, until the JCG cutter and helicopters mobilized by Ryoya's investigation arrive.

## 2. Post-Story Extras (Chronological Order)

- **Extra 1 [Hospital Recovery]**: Hina's Ship POV & Haruto's Visit *(Est. 500–650 lines)*
  - Covers Hina's semi-conscious state between Reika's assault and waking up to stop Ota, grounding her physical survival while featuring a single ambiguous, unnamed appearance of Kaori. Transitions to the hospital where Takahashi Haruto (29) visits; Hina keeps the vision to herself with a brief, lighthearted remark.
- **Extra 2 [Discharge → ~2 Months Post-Incident]**: Reika's Recovery *(Est. 450–600 lines)*
  - Reika (discharged earlier) bids goodbye to Hina at the hospital, then cuts forward ~2 months. Although her external life appears more stable, she still carries grief and guilt, working through ongoing trauma counseling with **Dr. An (Psychiatrist)**.
- **Extra 3 [~4 Months Post-Incident]**: *Kusabi* Reunion *(Est. 300–450 lines)*
  - All five *Kusabi* survivors (Ryoya, Nao, Risa, Kaito, Misaki) reunite ~4 months later. Ryoya excuses himself early because he promised his pregnant wife not to stay out late.

## 3. Character Voice Matrix

| Character | Psychic Distance & POV | Voice & Sentence Rhythm | Guardrails |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Shiraishi Reika (23)** | Close / Free Indirect | Guarded, self-pressuring, clipped when anxious, observant of small domestic/physical details. | Driven by worry for her father and survival, never sudden detective competence. |
| **Takahashi Hina (23)** | Close-Medium | Direct, polite university-student register, sharp at reading emotional masks, firm under pressure. | Treat intuitive sensitivity as physical/emotional unease, not supernatural exposition. |
| **Amamiya Nao (28)** | Medium (Mask) → Close | Playboy mask: breezy, teasing, deflective. True self: concise, analytical, grounded journalist. | Do not buff beyond investigative logic; relies on clues and makes real mistakes. |
| **Arai Ryoya (28)** | Medium-Far (Procedural) | Composed, methodical, evidence-bound, attentive to procedure and team input. | Must rely on Sōsa Nika teamwork and clash realistically with Saruwatari's rules. |
| **Ota Yuichi (28)** | External Threat Only | Silent or minimal speech; cold, patient predator exploiting blind spots. | No villain monologues; threat comes from quiet calculation. |
| **Nakamura / Murakami / Takai** | Grounded Supporting | Practical maritime/medical register focused on triage, containment, and evacuation. | Keep technical jargon concise so pacing stays urgent. |
