# Story Concept Specification: Project "Hizumi (歪み)"

**Status**: Active Master Overview (Supersedes `kusabi-house/work/crossover_concept_draft.md` v2.5)  
**Canon Year**: 2019–2020  
**Trilogy Position**: Final installment (*Tsukinomi* -> *Kusabi* -> *Hizumi*)

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Thematic Pillars
- **Genre**: Modern Sci-Fi + Psychological Supernatural Horror (*Man of Medan* style) interwoven with Corporate Economic Crime Police Procedural (Sōsa Nika).
- **Thematic Core — Human Hubris (The "Ultron" Principle)**:
  - Humanity creates advanced technology intended for revolutionary benefit (eliminating stress and seasickness), yet fundamentally lacks understanding or control over what it unleashes—building an uncontrollable force while treating it as a commercial tool.
- **Thematic Core — Psychological Distortion (歪み / Hizumi)**:
  - Rather than relying on a "chosen hero," the narrative centers on ordinary, flawed individuals trapped in an escalating catastrophe. Under cognitive stress and electromagnetic resonance, grief, guilt, workplace resentment, and fear warp perception until allies look like threats and delusion replaces reality.
- **The "Supernatural" Quality — The Inexplicable**:
  - Once collective cognitive filters break, phenomena occur that defy purely chemical or neurological explanations—shared delusions, impossible coincidences, and lingering echoes of the dead (*Kaori* in Extra 2)—leaving characters and readers to confront whether the machine merely triggered hallucinations or stripped away the veil between reality and the inexplicable.

## 2. Dual-Track Narrative Engine: Two Halves of a Single Truth

| Track | Setting & Tone | Primary POV & Agency | Narrative Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Track A (The Cruise Ship)** | Isolated vessel at sea; visceral terror, subjective distortion, claustrophobic survival | **Shiraishi Mao (23)**, supported by decentralized agency across Hina (23), Nao (28), First Officer Nakamura (36), and passengers | Delivers emotional stakes, interpersonal breakdown, and the physical struggle to jettison the machine before dawn on Day 5 |
| **Track B (Mainland — Sōsa Nika)** | Tokyo Metropolitan Police; objective logic, forensic paper trails, bureaucratic friction | **Arai Ryoya (28)**, working within a 5-person unit (Saruwatari, Takeda, Matsuoka, Kawazura) | Deciphers *why* the ship went dark, uncovers corporate cover-ups, and mobilizes Coast Guard rescue despite a Night 3–4 storm |

### Convergence at Dawn (Day 5, 06:00 AM)
- Track A survivors jettison the **AETHEL Core** via the Deck 2 Aft Marine Research Bay Moon Pool ramp before 06:00 AM, terminating the electromagnetic cavity resonance and hallucinations.
- With broadband jamming gone, First Officer Nakamura's primed **EPIRB** emergency beacon immediately transmits the ship's drifted coordinates, allowing the Coast Guard dispatched by Ryoya's Track B investigation to break through the clearing storm at 06:00 AM.

## 3. High-Level 5-Day Progression & Night 4 Architecture
1. **Day 1 (Normality & Gala)**: Luxury tech launch; character reunions (Mao, Hina, Koharu); Nao's undercover infiltration (drops coded notebook, overheard calling Ryoya); Shinji's parting words to Mao.
2. **Day 2 (Degradation & Runaway)**: Noon–evening signal degradation observed by Captain Hayasaka; Ota Yuichi suffers early core exposure and is sent to rest; Night 2 overclock causes thermal/harmonic runaway, locking the machine bay.
3. **Day 3 (Blackout & Unseen Losses)**: Early passenger-on-passenger killings begin; Captain attempts a discreet radio call before signals hit 0% blackout at Night 3; Shiraishi Shinji dies alone unseen (initiating Mao's hallucination arc); severe offshore storm begins.
4. **Day 4 (The "First Spark" & 3-Group Split)**:
   - *Evening Catalyst*: An acutely psychotic VIP stabs a crew member and leaps from the 4th-tier Grand Atrium balcony, shattering social order.
   - *Night 4 Split*: Survivors fracture into **3 Survival Groups + 1 Lone Hunter**:
     - **Group 1 (Escort)**: Hina, Koharu, Yamasaki (toxic senior, 2-step betrayal leading to his death), and lost 8-year-old Morita Sota.
     - **Group 2 (Psychological)**: Mao, Medical Assistant Takai Keisuke (27), and Sota's parents (Shuji & Kanae). Takai notices Mao talking to thin air; Mao snaps upon discovering her father died on Day 3.
     - **Group 3 (Command)**: Nao, Captain Hayasaka (dies in Night 4 accident), and First Officer Nakamura (holds the bridge/EPIRB while Nao descends to Deck 2).
     - **Lone Hunter**: Ota Yuichi (28), stalking maintenance blind spots as a cold, calculating killer.
   - *Night 4 Climax*: In the isolated Deck 2 Aft Service Corridor, broken Mao attacks Hina with an emergency industrial flashlight; Nao intervenes to save Hina and restore Mao's sanity; Ota ambushes them, injures Mao, and corners both before injured Hina incapacitates him.
5. **Day 5 (06:00 AM) & 3 Post-Story Extras**: Machine sunk -> Rescue arrives -> Extra 1 (*Kusabi* reunion + Ryoya's pregnant wife), Extra 2 (Hina's ship POV + ambiguous Kaori + Haruto's hospital visit), Extra 3 (Mao's hospital visit to Hina and moving forward).
