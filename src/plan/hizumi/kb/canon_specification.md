# Canon Specification: Project "Hizumi (歪み)"

## 1. Overview & Trilogy Placement
- **Title**: Hizumi (歪み / ความบิดเบี้ยว)
- **Position**: Final installment closing the main shared-universe trilogy (*Tsukinomi* -> *Kusabi* -> *Hizumi*).
- **Canon Year**: **2019–2020** (approximately 1 year after the 2018 closing scene of *Tsukinomi*, and 7–8 years after the 2012 events of *Kusabi*).
- **Genre**: Modern Sci-Fi + Psychological Supernatural Horror (*Man of Medan* style) interwoven with Corporate Economic Crime Police Procedural (Sōsa Nika).

## 2. Dual-Track Narrative Engine
- **Track A (The Cruise Ship — 5-Day Descent & Survival)**:
  - Begins as a luxury corporate technology launch gala.
  - Escalates from subtle signal degradation and Ota's early symptoms (Day 2) to a midday comms blackout, panic-driven passenger deaths, the engineering team's collapse, and the heroine's father's unseen death (Day 3).
  - Erupts into mass hysteria following a "First Spark" incident on Day 4 evening, splitting survivors into three distinct groups and one lone hunter. Amid spreading fires, explosions, and hull flooding, survivors fail to shut down the core directly and must evacuate via lifeboats with a portable EPIRB as the ship sinks on Day 5 morning.
- **Track B (Mainland — Sōsa Nika Investigation)**:
  - Tokyo Metropolitan Police Second Investigation Division (Sōsa Nika) audits a routine subsidiary accounting fraud case, exchanging early corporate hardware notes with Nao on Days 1–2 before comms drop mid-call at Day 3 noon.
  - Paper trails and a coerced/whistleblowing co-researcher confession expose prior suppressed incidents, lack of a remote kill-switch, and a lethal 72-hour threshold.
  - After overcoming internal procedural delays and coordinating with the Japan Coast Guard (JCG) through a severe Night 3–4 storm, rescue forces reach the lifeboat survivors 3–4 hours after dawn on Day 5 when the sinking ship's jamming clears and the portable EPIRB transmits.

## 3. Hard Canon Locks (Non-Negotiable Constraints)
1. **Age & Role Locks (2019–2020)**:
   - **Shiraishi Reika (formerly Mao)**: Age 23, female, 4th-year university student (same faculty as Hina).
   - **Takahashi Hina**: Age 23, female, 4th-year university student, intern/welcome MC.
   - **Amamiya Nao**: Age 28, male, undercover investigative journalist.
   - **Arai Ryoya**: Age 28, male, Sōsa Nika detective (5–6 years in the police force, 1–2 years in Sōsa Nika).
   - **Takahashi Haruto**: Age 29, male (appears in Extra 1).
2. **Protagonist Decentralization**:
   - Reika is an ordinary person caught in the disaster by misfortune, not a chosen hero. Survivors cannot disarm or destroy the AETHEL Core directly; survival and rescue depend on distributed agency across multiple characters (First Officer Nakamura, Nao, Hina, Dr. Murakami, Takai, the Sōsa Nika team, and supporting passengers).
3. **Heroine's Progressive Hallucination Arc**:
   - *Day 1–2*: Interacts with her living father, who leaves parting words in her memory (warning her to head toward the aft deck if the ship's hum ever sounds wrong).
   - *Day 3*: Father dies alone in a secluded Deck 2 breaker alcove without Reika or the reader knowing.
   - *Late Day 3–Day 4*: Reika's mind constructs a hallucination of her father, conversing with him when alone so the reader does not immediately suspect. Later, an observer (such as Takai Keisuke or Morita Kanae) asks who she is talking to.
   - *Night 4 Climax*: While Nao scouts ahead in the isolated Deck 2 service corridor and Hina watches Nao, Reika is lured by a hallucinated voice of her father to discover his Day 3 corpse. Her psyche collapses under peak wave interference; she violently attacks Hina with an improvised everyday object (heavy industrial flashlight), nearly killing Hina before Nao rushes back to restrain her and restore her sanity.

## 4. Post-Story Extras (3 Epilogue Chapters — Chronological Order)
- **Extra 1: Hina's Ship Perspective & Haruto's Hospital Visit (Medium Length)**:
  - Recounts Hina's semi-conscious state aboard the ship between Reika's assault and waking up to confront Ota, explaining both her physical adrenaline/survival plausibility and an ambiguous, unnamed appearance of **Kaori** helping her (left open for readers to interpret as Hina's subconscious or Kaori's lingering echo, without breaking *Tsukinomi* canon where Hina never met Kaori in person).
  - Transitions to the hospital shortly after Hina wakes up. **Takahashi Haruto (29)** visits her; Hina keeps the strange vision to herself, making a casual/teasing remark before talking normally with her brother.
- **Extra 2: Reika's Discharge & Two-Month Recovery (Medium Length)**:
  - Told from **Reika's POV**. Having sustained lighter injuries (from Ota), she is discharged from the hospital earlier and says goodbye to Hina.
  - Cuts forward to **~2 months post-incident**: although Reika's external life appears to be stabilizing, she still struggles internally with grief and guilt, working through ongoing recovery with **Dr. An (Psychiatrist)** rather than healing instantaneously on her own.
- **Extra 3: Kusabi Gang Reunion (~4 Months Post-Incident, Short & Concise)**:
  - All five *Kusabi* survivors (**Ryoya, Nao, Risa, Kaito, Misaki**) meet roughly 4 months after the cruise incident to catch up on their lives.
  - During conversation, Ryoya excuses himself early because he promised his pregnant wife he would not stay out late, naturally revealing his marriage and upcoming fatherhood.
