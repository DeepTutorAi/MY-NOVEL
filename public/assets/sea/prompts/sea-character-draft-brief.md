## Locked art direction

- **Use case:** `stylized-concept`
- **Asset type:** identity reference / character model sheet for future story illustrations
- **Style:** cinematic semi-realistic dark maritime fantasy concept art; grounded anatomy; painterly but clearly resolved facial features; weathered practical clothing; no glossy game-poster finish
- **Lighting:** cool cyan/teal overhead light derived from the suspended sea, with restrained warm amber edge light from the inhabited world below
- **Backdrop:** plain desaturated slate-grey studio field with a faint paper/canvas texture; no location, architecture, water ceiling, ship, or narrative scene
- **Sheet layout:** one consistent person shown as a front full-body view, a three-quarter full-body view, one face close-up, and three restrained expression studies; include a small detail inset only when a canonical hand, scar, mutation, or object matters
- **Identity lock:** all views must have the same facial structure, apparent age, body proportions, hair, eye color, skin tone, clothing, scars, and accessories
- **Text policy:** no names, labels, rulers, captions, signatures, logos, or watermark inside the image; canonical height and filename stay in this document
- **Framing:** every full-body view includes the top of the head and both feet; hands are visible when they carry character information
- **Avoid:** anime exaggeration, photoreal photography, beauty-editorial glamour, heroic bodybuilder anatomy, modern clothing, decorative fantasy armor, random weapons, extra people, duplicated limbs, cropped feet, readable text, wet underwater staging, blue skin unless canon requires it

## Draft workflow

1. Generate a versioned draft such as `cael-arvane-draft-v1.png`.
2. Inspect the full-resolution result for identity consistency, anatomy, clothing, canonical details, and accidental story additions.
3. Regenerate with one targeted correction when rejected; never overwrite a prior version.
4. After author approval, copy the accepted version to a stable production-reference filename outside `drafts/` in a separate integration pass.
5. Future scene generation must use the accepted sheet as an identity reference, not as an edit target.
