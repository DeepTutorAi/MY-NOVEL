# 🎙️ Voice & Style Principles: The Sea That Hung Above The World

This document defines the stylistic rules and voice guidelines for the project. Both human writers and AI agents must follow these rules to maintain stylistic coherence and avoid generic prose.

---

## 👁️ Narrative Stance & Perspective

- **Point of View**: Shifting Third-Person Limited (Cael, Ilyra, Sera, Nio, Maera).
- **POV Structure**: Locked at exactly 5 POV characters:
  - **Cael Arvane** (Active Present - Suppressed ENFJ): Focuses on moisture, rainfall frequencies, temperature, and signs of mutation (pale water-stains on wrists, breathing).
  - **Ilyra Venn** (Active Present - ENFP): Focuses on wind direction, rigging wear, ship weight, flight speeds, and the horizon.
  - **Maera Sol** (Active Present - ISTP): Focuses on mechanical structures, leverage, torque, friction, engine pressure, and Tovin's physical actions/injuries.
  - **Sera Quill** (Active Present - INTJ): Focuses on documents, ink age, map coordinates, official seals, and Ministry propaganda.
  - **Nio Arvane** (Past Flashbacks & Present Active - INFP):
    - *Past Flashbacks*: Human-focused, childhood memories, the cold sensation of the forbidden rain, and the horror of the Salt Ward containment cells.
    - *Present Active*: Mutated, non-human, abstract sensory filters. Focuses on whale song frequencies, bone-calcium vibrations, the thoughts of the Leviathan, and the shifting tides.
- **Psychic Distance**: Close/Deep POV. The narration must be filtered strictly through the active character's knowledge state, biases, and sensory limitations.
- **Observing the Leader**: To maximize Cassian Veyr's "aura" of leadership, he is strictly a non-POV character. His decisive actions, absolute calm under pressure, and mental anchor role are observed through the eyes of Maera and Sera (in Team Cassian) and Cael and Ilyra (in Team Cael), making his competence feel mythic and earned rather than narrated in the first-person.

---

## ✍️ Prose Rhythm & Sentence Patterns

- **Sentence Variety**:
  - Short, fragmented sentences for moments of body horror, panic, or claustrophobia (e.g., *Metal in the throat. Breath caught. A sound like drowning.*).
  - Longer, flowing sentences when describing the vast, terrifying beauty of the Sky Ocean or characters reflecting on their past.
- **Show vs. Tell**:
  - Avoid abstract emotional labels like *"He was afraid,"* *"She felt sad,"* or *"They were shocked."*
  - Instead, show fear and sadness through perception, avoidance, and physical reaction (e.g., *His collar felt damp. He didn't look at the glove. The pale stain spread at the corner of his eye, stiffening as it dried.*).
  - Filter words to avoid: *saw, heard, felt, realized, noticed, knew*. Write the observation directly (e.g., write *A whale song shook the deck* instead of *She heard a whale song shake the deck*).

## 🎨 Sensory Grounding & Details

- **Primary Sensory Anchors**:
  - **Scent**: Sulfur, rotting kelp, wet wood, metallic copper, the ozone of deep water.
  - **Sound**: The dull vibration of whale songs through the hull, the drip of condensation, the wind whistling through rigging, the crackle of drying brine on skin.
  - **Touch**: The weight of damp clothes, the cold, smooth glass of Nio's mutated skin, the itch of old water-stains.
- **Rule of Thumb**: Select one or two highly specific sensory details for each new setting rather than listing everything. What the character notices must reveal who they are.

## 🗣️ Dialogue & Subtext

- **Dialogue Friction**: Characters should rarely state their exact feelings or motivations. Use dialogue to hide secrets, lie, or test boundaries.
- **Tags & Beats**:
  - Minimize dialogue tags (e.g. *said, asked*). Use action beats to ground the characters in their physical task (e.g., *Ilyra pulled the knot tight. "We need to move."* instead of *"We need to move," she said, pulling the knot.*).
  - Never use dramatic dialogue tags (*shouted, demanded, wept, explained*). Let the words and beats convey the emotion.

### Strict Prohibition Against Overly Clipped, Staccato & Cryptic Dialogue
- **No Robotic Truncation**: Dialogue must never be reduced to telegraphic single-word replies or fragmented checklists (e.g., answering `"ยัง"`, `"ตก"`, `"ปิด?"`, `"ไว้ก่อน"`, `"อืม"`, or repeating empty `"ครับ"`). Such clipped exchanges obscure plot causality, damage emotional chemistry, and turn human characters into lifeless NPCs.
- **Subtext vs. Obscurity**: Subtext is the dramatic tension between what is meant and what is said. It is **not** the mechanical omission of essential words or conversational context. If the reader cannot understand who is speaking, what is being discussed, or what physical choice is at stake, the dialogue has failed.
- **Mandatory Flow & Breathing Particles**: Spoken Thai relies inherently on flow particles (`ได้`, `ก็`, `อยู่`, `ไว้`, `ไป`, `มา`, `นะ`, `หรอก`, `ล่ะ`, `มั้ง`, `สิ`, `เนอะ`, `ซะ`) to signal interpersonal distance, nuance, and emotion. Stripping these particles produces wooden, translation-ese text. Even guarded or taciturn characters must use appropriate flow particles to sound like living human beings.

### Character Voice Differentiation
- **Cael Arvane**: Short, hesitant sentences, uses language only when necessary. Quiet, polite, under immense pressure.
  - **CRITICAL**: Being "quiet" (`พูดน้อย`) means speaking *infrequently* and with restraint; it **does NOT mean answering in robotic single words or cryptic fragments** (e.g. answering `"ยัง"`, `"ไว้ก่อน"`, `"ตก"`, `"ปิด?"`). When Cael speaks, his sentences are complete, thoughtful, and humble, utilizing natural particles (`ครับ`, `ล่ะ`, `หรอก`, `ดีกว่าครับ`).
- **Ilyra Venn**: Rapid, colloquial speech, talks to cover her anxiety. Defiant, sharp, and humorous. Uses `ฉัน-เธอ` or `ฉัน-นาย`.
  - **CRITICAL**: Ilyra is a modern dieselpunk/airship captain, not a medieval monarch or wuxia heroine. She is **strictly forbidden from using `ข้า` or addressing crew members with `เจ้า`**.
- **Sera Quill**: Precise, analytical vocabulary, speaks in terms of coordinates, records, and concrete facts. Sharp-tongued. Uses `ฉัน` in private and feminine polite endings in formal speech.
- **Maera Sol**: Prickly, dry, and highly technical. Refuses to use emotional words (like *worry, care, love*). When concerned, she scolds. Uses mechanical terms (*torque, clearance, fuel, friction*). Uses `ฉัน-นาย` with Tovin and `ฉัน-เธอ` with Ilyra.
  - **CRITICAL**: Maera is **strictly forbidden from using `ข้า` or `เจ้า`**. She never addresses elders with fantasy-translation clichés like `"เตาของเจ้า"`.
- **Tovin**: Simple, warm, and self-deprecating. Uses ordinary language, focuses on human needs (*soup, sleep, heat*). Acts as a conversational contrast to the grand, poetic vocabulary of the mythic characters.
  - **CRITICAL**: Tovin speaks warm, grounded colloquial Thai (`ฉัน-นาย`, `ฉัน-เธอ`, and `ผม` with elders/general public). Tovin is **strictly forbidden from using `ข้า` or `เจ้า`**. He is a village youth and mechanic's mate, never an ancient warrior.
- **Cassian**: ENTJ Analyst. Decisive, strategic, and authoritative. Speaks in terms of large-scale logistics, tactical advantages, and macro-objectives. He avoids emotional pleading, speaking with calm, quiet authority. His actions define him. His humor is deadpan and unintentional, stemming from extreme literalism. He treats civilian banter, sarcasm, and Tovin's blockheaded attempts at romance/mechanics with serious tactical analysis, creating natural situational comedy.
- **Loran**: ESTJ Sentinel. Authoritative, direct, and highly organized. Speaks using precise aviation and military navigation terminology (*"vector," "bearing," "payload," "descent"*). He views duty as a concrete responsibility to his dependents rather than an abstract ideal; his speech pattern is blunt, demanding order, and rejecting Cassian's ENTJ strategic martyrdom as an illogical waste of leadership.

## 🧭 Language, Names & Register

- **Narrative language**: Thai prose; established proper names remain in English/Latin script (`Cael`, `Nio`, `Albatross`, `Sera`). Thai transliterations may appear only as deliberate in-world nicknames or quoted local usage, not as random alternates.
- **Industrial-fantasy register**: Metric units, gauges, pressure, screens, nylon, and other established technologies are allowed because this is a post-engineering world, not a medieval one. Prefer the Thai functional term when it is equally precise; retain English only for proper nouns or technical terms whose foreignness matters in-world.
- **Strict Character Pronoun Rules**:
  - **Cael & Tovin**: Default strictly to `ผม` in narrative interiority, public interaction, and when speaking with elders or officials. In informal exchanges between peers (e.g. Tovin speaking with Maera or Ilyra), Tovin uses `ฉัน-เธอ` or `ฉัน-นาย`. **Tovin is strictly forbidden from using `ข้า` under any circumstances.**
  - **Ilyra & Maera**: Default strictly to `ฉัน` for first-person and `เธอ` / `นาย` for second-person. **Both Ilyra and Maera are strictly forbidden from using `ข้า` or addressing anyone as `เจ้า`.**
  - **Sera**: Uses `ฉัน` in private and feminine polite endings in formal speech.
  - **Cassian & Loran**: Default to `ผม`; any switch to `ฉัน` must mark a clearly motivated loss of formal distance.
  - **Permitted Peer Register**: `แก` เป็นคู่ร่วมวัยที่อนุญาตระหว่าง Ilyra/Maera/Tovin/Lisse เท่านั้น (ตัวบทสถาปนาตั้งแต่บทที่ 18-20 — อนุมัติโดยเจ้าของเรื่อง 2026-11-07)
  - **Prohibition on Archaic Pronouns**: Words such as `ข้า`, `เจ้า`, `ข้าพเจ้า`, and archaic honorifics like `ขอรับ` or `ท่าน` (when addressing peers/friends) are completely banned from character dialogue.

- **Prohibited Archaic, Period-Drama & Translation-ese Register (Banned Vocabulary)**:
  - **Banned Feudal & Period-Drama Terms**:
    - `ข้า-เจ้า` (use `ฉัน-เธอ`, `ฉัน-นาย`, `ผม-คุณ`)
    - `ขอรับ` (use `ครับ`)
    - `ธุด / ธุดหิน` (misused Buddhist monastic term; use `ม้านั่งหิน`, `แท่นหินหน้าร้าน`, `ลานหิน`)
    - `ธรณีประตู` (banned by POV register; use `ขอบประตู`, `หน้าประตู`)
    - `กินเสียสิ / กินเสียหน่อยสิ` (archaic feudal imperative; use `กินสิ`, `กินหน่อยน่า`, `กินเข้าไปเถอะ`)
    - `ไม่เอื้อนเอ่ยวาจา` (costume drama prose; use `ยืนนิ่งเงียบ`, `ไม่พูดอะไร`)
    - `จาร` (misused palm-leaf religious term; use `สลัก`, `เขียน`, `จด`)
    - `ส่องดาบ` (distorted action; use `ชักดาบ`)
    - `ชะงัดนักเชียว`, `พ่อหนุ่ม`, `Dama Venn` (foreign/archaic titles without world context)
  - **Banned Malapropisms & Distorted Collocations**:
    - `คุ้ยไอน้ำ / คุ้ยแรงดัน / กองเพลิงที่ยังคุ้ย` (misused verb `คุ้ย`; use `คุ้ยถ่านเลี้ยงแรงดัน`, `เลี้ยงแรงดันเตา`, `กองเพลิงที่ยังคุกรุ่น`)
    - `หนังสติ๊กหิน` (childish/distorted term for heavy demolition tools; use `เครื่องกระทุ้งหิน`, `แท่นกวาดล้างศิลา`)
    - `มันคาว` (distorted typo/malapropism for worn smooth leather; use `มันวาว`, `ขึ้นเงา`)
    - `ข้อหักขาว / ข้อหักขาวระริก` (bizarre calque of white knuckles; use `ข้อนิ้วขาวซีด`, `กำหมัดจนข้อนิ้วขึ้นรอยขาว`)
    - `ขวดแก้วลำต้นเดียวกัน` (nonsensical translation; use `แก้วเนื้อเดียวกัน`, `เบ้าหลอมเดียวกัน`)
    - `ล้อเรือ` (for an airship without wheels; use `พังงาเรือ`, `คันบังคับ`)
    - `ดังตุ๊ด ๆ` (inappropriate onomatopoeia for creaking wooden ship ribs; use `ดังเอี๊ยดอ๊าด`, `ดังกรอบแกรบ`)
    - `ปีกโปร่งแสงเหมือนเนื้อปอด` (biologically nonsensical simile; use `ปีกโปร่งแสงเป็นริ้วบางคล้ายเส้นใยแมงกะพรุน`)
  - **Banned Translation-ese Calques (สำนวนแปลภาษาอังกฤษทื่อๆ)**:
    - `สามารถเปลี่ยนแปลงคนได้` (from *could change people*; use `แปรสภาพร่างกายมนุษย์ให้กลายเป็นสิ่งอื่น`)
    - `คนที่หลับพร้อมกันทั้งลำคือคนแรกที่จะพาเรือไปตาย` (garbled calque; use `ถ้าปล่อยให้ทุกคนหลับพร้อมกันเมื่อไหร่ ก็เตรียมตัวตายกันทั้งลำ`)
    - `เย็นกว่า...ก้าวหนึ่งเสมอ` (from *one step cooler*; use `เย็นกว่ามือของผมอยู่เสมอนิดหน่อย`)
    - `การฆ่าทุกคนแพง` (from *killing everyone is expensive*; use `การสังหารกวาดล้างทั้งหมู่บ้านมีต้นทุนสูงเกินไป`)
    - `นอนให้สุด อย่าโชว์ความอุ่น` (nonsensical literal translation; use `รีบนอนเอาแรงซะ อย่ามัวมาห่วงคนอื่น`)
    - `ร่องรอย...ไหลซึมและไหลรวม` (category error; traces cannot flow; use `หยาดน้ำเค็มและไอน้ำชื้นแฉะไหลซึม...`)
    - `สบถพึมพำเรื่องเส้นผมที่ไม่เคยยอมเชื่อฟังใคร` (from *hair that never obeyed anyone*; use `บ่นพึมพำว่าผมชี้ฟูไม่เคยยอมเรียบเหมือนคนอื่นเขา`)

- **Government terms**: `สภาศิลาไร้ฝน` is the ruling council, `กระทรวง` its executive bureaucracy, and `หมู่หินไร้หยดน้ำ` its emblem/seal.
- **Salt Ward terms**: `Salt Ward`, `White-Silt Bastion`, `ปราการผลึกขาว`, and `ปราการขาว` name the same complex in different registers. `สถานบำบัดและฟื้นฟูสภาวะเปียก` is its archival euphemism.
