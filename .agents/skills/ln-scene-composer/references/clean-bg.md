# Background budget and active contract

Resolve roles in [production-workflow.md](production-workflow.md). Preserve
referenced palette, linework and setting intent unless the brief changes them.
Atmosphere can be a co-subject; background need not always be nearly empty.

## Background Levels: presets

| Level | Purpose | Starting treatment |
|---|---|---|
| 0, tone only | Isolated portrait/emotion | Quiet tone/gradient and source-compatible separation |
| 1, suggested place | Little setting emphasis | One or two restrained location cues |
| 2, atmospheric context | Narration, time/weather and character together | Readable setting reduced to useful shapes with clear focal hierarchy |

A setting reference or atmosphere-focused/detailed brief can justify richer
context. These levels are not a ceiling. Do not require the literal words
"detailed background" or remove needed architecture/furniture to satisfy a
universal two-prop rule.

## Resolve the active Background Contract

State place/story cues, time/weather/light, required elements, focal hierarchy,
detail/contrast and source-consistent separation. Count held story props
separately. Preserve requested object counts/owners.

A quiet portrait from zero can use the default paragraph below verbatim.
A scenic/reference-led job uses its resolved contract instead; do not paste
contradictory contracts together. White outlines/halos are source-dependent.

## DEFAULT CLEAN BG LOCK

```text
Background: keep it minimal and quiet. Most of the frame behind the characters is empty soft tone or a screentone gradient, with at most two simple props drawn in thin, light lines that are clearly lighter and thinner than the characters' lines. Leave large areas of the frame empty. A thin white gap separates the characters from the background. Do not fill the scene with buildings, foliage, furniture, crowds, shelves, patterns or fine texture.
```

## Repair demonstrated clutter

Identify what competes with the intended subject/action or creates an accidental
tangent. Reduce that element's detail/contrast or revise the view, retaining
required place/atmosphere cues. An environment extending off-frame is valid.
Preserve source layout for a local edit; do not lower a Level merely because a
default is quieter. Reuse the active contract across candidates/panels unless
the user changes it, and judge QC against that contract.
