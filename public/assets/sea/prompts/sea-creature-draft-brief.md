## Locked art direction — Sea Above Gothic Maritime Body Horror

- **Use case:** `stylized-concept`
- **Asset type:** creature, ecology, or infected-state identity sheet for future illustrated-fiction scenes
- **Core style:** cinematic semi-realistic dark maritime survival concept art; Bloodborne-influenced silhouette discipline and tragic transformation; Lovecraftian scale, partial revelation, and cosmic unease; original Sea Above biology and material language rather than copied franchise designs
- **Body-horror rule:** preserve the recognizable original animal or human beneath the transformation. Horror comes from adaptation, memory, calcification, parasitism, and loss of bodily ownership—not random gore.
- **Biology rule:** every growth, organ, shell, wound, light, or deformity must support the creature's behavior, origin, survival method, or narrative function.
- **Lighting:** cool cyan/teal overhead light derived from the suspended sea, with restrained amber rim light from the inhabited world below
- **Backdrop:** plain desaturated slate-grey studio field with faint paper/canvas texture; environmental fragments only when required to explain scale or behavior
- **Creature-sheet layout:** one consistent specimen shown in readable side, three-quarter, and behavioral views; one large head or defining-organ close-up; one or two anatomy/material details; no labels inside the image
- **Infected-human layout:** one consistent person shown before or at the earliest recognizable state, the canonical infected state, a face close-up that preserves human awareness where canon requires it, and restrained mutation details. Keep clothing sufficient to preserve dignity and avoid specimen-porn staging.
- **Identity lock:** all views of a specimen or infected person must preserve the same anatomy, proportions, damage, growth pattern, coloration, and stage of transformation
- **Text policy:** no names, labels, captions, rulers, signatures, logos, or watermark inside the image
- **Body-horror restraint:** no decorative blood spray, exposed brain, loose organs, sexualized suffering, torture tableau, or gore without narrative function. Body horror may be intense but must remain story-specific and materially coherent.
- **Mystery rule:** the Drowned Choir, Choir-Warden, First Leviathan, and other partial-reveal entities must not be shown more completely than their approved reveal stage.
- **Avoid:** generic tentacle monster; every creature sharing the same eyes or mouth; copied Bloodborne enemy; copied Lovecraft illustration; generic zombie; Resident Evil mutant-dog silhouette; demonic horns without biology; gothic armor; random chains; skull decoration unrelated to anatomy; anime monster; glossy game-poster finish; extra specimens; inconsistent anatomy; cropped defining features; readable text

## Draft workflow

1. Generate and review exactly one package at a time.
2. Save every candidate under `public/assets/sea/images/creatures/drafts/` with a versioned filename.
3. Inspect full resolution for canon, biological coherence, body-horror specificity, silhouette, anatomy, repeated identity, accidental text, and reveal-boundary violations.
4. Regenerate with one targeted correction when rejected. Never overwrite an earlier version.
5. Do not begin the next creature until the author accepts the current candidate or explicitly redirects the queue.
6. Approved drafts remain references only. Production integration is a separate pass.

## Threat escalation campaign — V2 direction

The original creature run established materials, conditions, and named silhouettes, but its sheet-led framing often made danger look observational rather than immediate. The V2 campaign is an additive, non-destructive direction: every new file preserves its V1 predecessor and must answer one concrete fear question—what does the target notice too late, how does the threat close space or transfer harm, and which material/behavior separates it from the other creatures? Infected assets should retain recognizable humanity but lose safety through wrong movement, trapped attention, transmission risk, or bodily function. Predator assets should make hunting method, scale, and decision-making legible without substituting generic boss combat. The first wave is Ash-Turned, Coral Husk, Trench-Wyrm, and Void-Angler; all four are pending author review.
