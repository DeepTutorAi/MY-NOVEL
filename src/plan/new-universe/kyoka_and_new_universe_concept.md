# New Universe Project — Story Concept & Kyōka Character Bible
> **Working Title**: New Universe (Alternate Universe Spin-off)  
> **Genre**: Slice-of-Life Ensemble Drama / Psychological Character Study (*Clannad* / Key Visual Novel Style)  
> **Universe Continuity**: Separate Alternate Universe (AU) from the *Tsukinomi* → *Kusabi* → *Hizumi* trilogy. In this world, **Takahashi Haruto** and **Mizushima Kaori** lived together as a married couple and raised two children (**Kyōka** and **Ren**), though Haruto and Kaori remain mostly in the background to let the new generation and their friends drive the story.

---

## 1. Core Structure & *Clannad*-Style Ensemble Balance
* **Narrative Engine**:
  * Follows a central **Male Protagonist (POV)**—a high school student and close friend of **Ren**.
  * Balances daily school life and comedy with deep personal drama across multiple characters. Every character in the ensemble has their own distinct emotional wound, route/arc, and escalating crisis that the protagonist becomes involved in.
  * **Kyōka** and **Ren** are NOT the sole focus of the entire novel; during other characters' routes, they function as organic part of the ensemble. Kyōka has limited, high-impact screen time outside her own arc, appearing mainly when the protagonist and Ren visit her apartment for advice or when she drops cryptic, high-IQ hints that only make sense chapters later.

## 3. Kyōka's Deep Psychology, Skills & Realistic Grounding

### 3.1 The Cognitive Flaw: Understanding Mechanics vs. Irrational Emotion
* **How Her Genius Works**:
  * She does not lecture or show off academic trivia. Her 150+ IQ manifests as **instant systemic comprehension**, **anomaly detection across incomplete data**, and **multi-step predictive behavioral modeling**.
  * When she gives advice or warnings to the protagonist, she says things that seem cryptic or unrelated in the moment—because she is already three steps ahead. Other characters only realize what she meant much later when events unfold exactly as she foresaw.
* **The Psychological Blind Spot**:
  * She accurately perceives *what* people are feeling (fear, jealousy, self-deception) and understands the logical cause-and-effect of human behavior.
  * However, her Ti-dominant mindset cannot fathom **why humans still choose self-destructive paths even after knowing the outcome**. To her: *"If you know doing X will hurt you, why not simply stop?"*
  * Because she spoke with unfiltered, surgical honesty during her school days and was impossible to read or approach casually, most peers disliked or feared her.

### 3.2 Financial Independence, Foreign Travel & Jiu-Jitsu (Plot-Hole Proofing)
* **Why She Has Money & Her Own Apartment**:
  * Having competed internationally in her youth (in both **youth international Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu / martial arts** and **international academic/strategy competitions**), she accrued prize money and now works remotely from her apartment as a high-paying freelance quantitative analyst / system vulnerability researcher (bug bounty) and specialized technical translator.
  * This gives her a strong bank statement, complete financial independence without relying on Haruto or Kaori, and a valid Japanese passport with prior travel history—allowing her to book a flight and enter visa-free countries (Europe/North America/Asia allow 90–180 days visa-free for Japanese passport holders with sufficient funds) at a moment's notice.
* **Combat Capability**:
  * Like *Tomoyo Sakagami* (*Clannad*) but quieter and more surgical, Kyōka is trained in **Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu (BJJ)** and close-quarters striking, combining physical technique with spatial calculation.

## 4. Kyōka's Route / Arc: The 4-Stage Escalation Blueprint

### Stage 1 (The Backstory & Trigger 1 — The "Fake Date" & The Slap)
* **The Past Wound**: In high school, when everyone else avoided Kyōka, one girl and her friend group reached out and became Kyōka's only close friends. Later, when that best friend was spiraling, Kyōka delivered a blunt, unfiltered truth meant to snap her out of it—but the friend died shortly after, and the friend group shattered, blaming Kyōka's "heartless words" for her death.
* **Trigger 1A (Encounter in Town)**: While walking in town with the protagonist (whom she teases with deadpan humor by calling it a "date"), they run into her former high school friend group. Seeing Kyōka seemingly living well and "dating" someone triggers their resentment, leading them to harass her and dredge up her friend's death.
* **Trigger 1B (The Unresisted Slap)**: The protagonist witnesses a woman connected to the deceased friend (a relative or former group member) confront Kyōka outside and **slap her hard across the face** before walking away. Despite having the reflexes to dodge effortlessly, Kyōka stands still and takes the slap, lowering her head slightly while keeping her expression completely blank—silently accepting the punishment out of deep-seated guilt.

### Stage 2 (Trigger 2 — Ren's Fury & Severe Injury)
* **The Escalation**: Ren discovers that his sister is being harassed, slandered, and blamed for the past tragedy. Unable to tolerate them hurting Kyōka, Ren drops his usual goofy persona and confronts the group alone to defend her honor.
* Outnumbered, Ren is brutally beaten and hospitalized in critical condition.

### Stage 3 (Trigger 3 — The Ambush, The BJJ Counter-Attack, and The Vanishing Act)
* **Misdirected Guilt & Cold Rage**: Seeing Ren badly injured in the hospital because of her breaks something inside Kyōka. Blaming herself yet having nowhere else to channel her overwhelming anger, she goes alone to confront the perpetrators.
* **The Realistic Fight Sequence (Refs 1, 6, 7)**:
  1. Kyōka corners the female instigator, who breaks down begging for her life. In truth, Kyōka never intended to physically harm the girl—only to terrify her into never touching Ren again.
  2. In that split second of hesitation when her guard drops toward the crying girl, a male accomplice ambushes Kyōka from behind, **striking her head with a blunt weapon**.
  3. Kyōka falls to the ground, blood running from a gash above her right eyebrow (**Ref 6**), and the men kick her while she is down, tearing the shoulder of her shirt.
  4. Regaining her footing and composure, Kyōka uses her intellect to funnel them in the narrow space and executes a brutal **Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu leg lock (breaking the attacker's leg)**, transitions into a **full mount**, and rains down strikes on the man who ambushed her until he is on the verge of death—before stopping herself at the final moment (**Ref 7**).
* **The Decision to Erase Herself**:
  * After spending several days alone in her apartment, Kyōka concludes that her existence brings ruin to anyone close to her (first her high school friend, now Ren).
  * She systematically cuts all ties, terminates her lease, and prepares to leave Japan entirely so that everyone will forget her.

### Stage 4 (Climax & Resolution — The Snowy Pursuit with Kaori & Family Reunion)
* **Tracking Her Down Together with Kaori (Ref 2 & Ref 5)**:
  * Using a subtle clue Kyōka left behind (such as the framed photo of a snowy town on her bookshelf from when she competed abroad in **Ref 2**), the protagonist deduces where Kyōka is heading.
  * The protagonist does not go alone—**Kaori (Kyōka's mother)** accompanies him to find her.
  * When they intercept Kyōka amidst the falling snow (**Ref 5**), the protagonist steps back to let **Kyōka and Kaori have a quiet, pivotal mother-daughter conversation** first. Kaori's gentle, unwavering understanding pierces through Kyōka's cold logical defenses in a way pure debate never could.
* **The Emotional Catharsis — Returning to Ren & Haruto (Ref 8)**:
  * After her conversation with Kaori, Kyōka returns home/to the hospital with Kaori and the protagonist to reunite with **Ren** and **Haruto**.
  * Seeing her brother and father waiting for her, and enveloped in the embrace of her entire family (**Ren, Kaori, and Haruto**), Kyōka's stoic ISTP armor shatters completely; she bites her lip and weeps openly in their arms (**Ref 8**), finally forgiving herself.
